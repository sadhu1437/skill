Non-Engineering IT Role

System & App Services Associate

Accenture - Let there be change

PAN India Locations | 24x7 Shifts | Full-Time

Drive Overview

Accenture is a leading global professional services company that helps the world's leading businesses, governments, and organizations build their digital core and optimize their operations. We are currently seeking System and Application Services Associates to join our dynamic teams across India. This specific hiring drive is an exclusive opportunity for Non-Engineering graduates (B.Sc., BCA, BBA, B.A, B.Com) to kick-start a thriving career in IT operations, cloud support, and low-code app development.

Target Audience Non-Engineering Grads
Target Batches Till 2024 Passouts
Experience Level 0 - 23 Months Max

What You Will Do

You will work in challenging and dynamic environments, using your versatility to create and support tech solutions from analysis to implementation.

1. Infra Managed Services & Cloud Support

  • Manage infrastructure services and operations for on-premises and cloud-based technologies.
  • Support, monitor, and maintain applications and cloud computing systems securely.
  • Apply technical knowledge to diagnose and resolve application issues, continually improving operational effectiveness.

2. Low/No Code App Development

  • Develop applications using low-code platforms, setting up and configuring systems for unique client requirements.
  • Work with form builders, pre-built templates, drag-and-drop visual interfaces, and entity builders.
  • Research and analyze internal business processes to optimize and automate them.

3. Quality Engineering & Project Control

  • Create test designs, processes, test cases, and test data for usability testing.
  • Analyze testing results and submit actionable observations to development teams.
  • Support Technology Delivery Centers by tracking project management office (PMO) activities and execution of deliverables.

Strict Eligibility Criteria

Important: This drive explicitly EXCLUDES engineering streams. B.E/B.Tech/ME/M.Tech and MCA/M.Sc (CS/IT/Data Science) candidates are NOT ELIGIBLE.
B.Sc / BCA B.Com / BBA / B.A 0 - 23 Months Exp No Active Backlogs No Education Gaps
  • Degrees Allowed: Non-engineering graduates / post-graduates such as B.Sc., BCA, BBA, B.A, B.Com, etc.
  • Batch: Any year of passout till 2024.
  • Experience Cap: Candidates must not have more than 23 months of full-time experience.

  • Backlogs: No active backlogs during the application and/or onboarding phase.
  • Cooling Period: You should not have appeared for an Accenture assessment/interview in the last 3 months.

The Accenture Selection Roadmap

Accenture's hiring process for this role focuses heavily on aptitude, basic technical awareness, and communication skills:

1. Cognitive & Technical Assessment

A multi-part test comprising English Ability, Critical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, and basic Network Security/Cloud fundamentals. You must clear the Cognitive section to proceed.

2. Communication Assessment

A non-technical, AI-driven vocal test lasting ~20 minutes. It evaluates Sentence Mastery, Vocabulary, Fluency, and Pronunciation. Ensure you take this in a completely quiet room with a good headset.

3. Final Interview

Expect questions on your adaptability, problem-solving mindset, and willingness to learn new technologies (like Low-Code platforms). Be prepared to discuss any academic projects or operational experiences.

Expert Preparation Tips

Since this is a non-engineering role, the interviewers are not expecting deep coding knowledge. Instead, focus on demonstrating logical thinking and tech-savviness. Familiarize yourself with basic terms like Cloud Computing, Infrastructure Management, and Software Testing Lifecycles (STLC). Emphasize your communication skills and readiness to work in a 24x7 global operational environment.
