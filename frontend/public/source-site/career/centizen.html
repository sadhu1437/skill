Software Engineer

Centizen - Innovating Real AI-Powered Products

Location: Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu | Category: Development

Drive Overview

Centizen is actively looking for talented, driven engineers to join our dynamic team in Tirunelveli. We specialize in building and scaling real AI-powered products involving content generation, smart search, and automation. If you are a fresher or have up to 3 years of experience and are eager to learn and grow within the AI, Cloud, and Full-stack development space, this is a golden opportunity to launch your career. We believe in learning by solving actual business problems with modern technologies.

Experience Level 0 - 3 Years
Primary Domain AI / Cloud / Full-Stack
Location Tirunelveli, TN

Your Role & Responsibilities

You won't be restricted to a single technology. At Centizen, you will work across the stack to build real customer-facing products from the ground up.

  • AI Product Development: Work on real AI-powered products involving content generation, smart search algorithms, and automation workflows.
  • Full-Stack Features: Build and optimize scalable backend & frontend features for a seamless user experience.
  • Cloud Systems: Develop and deploy cloud-based systems tailored for robust performance.
  • Business Problem Solving: Learn continuously by solving actual business problems with modern technologies.
  • Career Growth: Transition into advanced full-time roles through our structured training and fast career progression programs.

Eligibility Criteria

B.E / B.Tech CSE / IT / AI / ML MCA / M.Sc IT 0-3 Years Exp Logical Thinking
  • Education: B.E/B.Tech (CSE, IT, AI/ML), MCA, M.Sc IT or equivalent degree.
  • Experience: 0 to 3 years of experience (Freshers & Experienced candidates can apply).
  • Programming: Basic programming knowledge in any language (e.g., Python, Java, JavaScript, C++).
  • Mindset: Eager to learn AI, Cloud & Full-stack development with strong logical thinking & problem-solving skills.

Market Trend: AI & Full-Stack Convergence

The tech industry is moving past traditional web development into the era of AI-Augmented Applications. Companies want engineers who can not only build a React frontend or a Node backend but also integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) for content generation or smart search. By starting your career at Centizen, you will gain hands-on exposure to these high-demand "GenAI + Full-Stack" skills, making your profile exceptionally competitive in the global market.

Centizen 5-Stage Selection Process

Our hiring process is designed to evaluate your fundamental logic, technical agility, and team fit:

Step 1: Application Screening

Shortlisting of candidate profiles based on academic background, relevant skills, and eagerness to work with AI technologies.

Step 2: Programming Written Test

An initial test focusing on core programming concepts, data structures, and algorithms to gauge your basic coding proficiency.

Step 3: Technical Evaluation

A deeper dive into your programming logic, understanding of web architecture, and how you approach problem-solving.

Step 4: Group Discussion

Assessment of your communication skills, teamwork, and ability to articulate technical or business ideas clearly.

Step 5: Final Interview & Offer

A face-to-face or virtual discussion focusing on cultural fit, your passion for building products, and career aspirations.

Expert Preparation Guide for Centizen

1. Programming Foundations

Since the role accepts "any language," pick your strongest language (e.g., Python or JavaScript) and master the basics: Object-Oriented Programming, string/array manipulation, and basic data structures. You will need these for the Programming Written Test.

2. AI Integration Awareness

The JD emphasizes "content generation" and "smart search." Spend an hour understanding how an API request is made to an AI service (like OpenAI or HuggingFace) and how you might display that data on a frontend interface.

3. Group Discussion Tactics

In the Group Discussion round, it's not just about dominating the conversation. Listen actively, summarize points made by others, and introduce structured, logical arguments. Focus on collaboration over competition.
