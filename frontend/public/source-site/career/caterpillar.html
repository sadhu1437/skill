Your Role & Responsibilities

This role bridges the gap between raw data operations and strategic business solutions. You will ensure data accuracy while implementing technologies that improve the digital capabilities of Caterpillar's enterprise systems.

  • Data Management: Input data into the customer data files, making changes to existing data and rigorously monitoring the accuracy of all data.
  • Solution Development: Develop detailed, data-driven solutions to solve recurring business and operational problems.
  • Technology Deployment: Participate in evaluating and deploying new technologies to add or enhance existing digital technical capabilities.
  • Requirements Analysis: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather, elicit, and record business functionality requirements for software development projects.
  • Customer Focus: Research and verify customer needs, address business requirements, and solicit satisfaction feedback to improve processes continuously.

Expert Preparation Guide for Caterpillar

1. Data Architecture & SQL Fundamentals

Since this role involves maintaining data files and analyzing data architecture, expect technical questions around SQL queries, Data Normalization, and ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) concepts. Brush up on how relational databases operate within an enterprise environment.

2. Requirements Elicitation Techniques

The job description heavily emphasizes "Requirements Analysis". Be prepared to explain how you would gather requirements from a non-technical stakeholder. Understand the software development life cycle (SDLC) and how business analysts use user stories and functional documentation.

3. The STAR Method for Behavioral Questions

Caterpillar values "Customer Focus" and "Effective Communications". Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to talk about a time you solved a complex problem using data, or a time you handled constructive feedback without being defensive.
