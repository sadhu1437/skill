Shell Graduate Programme 2026

Shell India - Powering Progress and Developing Future Leaders

Req ID: R182105 | Start Dates: May & August 2026

Drive Overview

Shell India is a powerhouse energy company with deep roots in R&D, digitalisation, and business operations. With over 13,000 employees in India, Shell is inviting the best and brightest minds to join its 3-year Graduate Programme. This isn't just an entry-level job; it's a leadership journey that combines on-the-job training, formal education, and job rotations to help you achieve your full potential while solving some of the world's most critical energy challenges.

Locations
Bangalore Gurgaon Mumbai Taloja
Eligibility Bachelor's / Master's / PhD
Worker Type Regular Full-Time

The Shell Graduate Experience

At Shell, you are given exciting responsibilities right from the start. You will work with experienced colleagues to share unique perspectives on global energy challenges.

  • Rotational Learning: Experience different facets of the energy business through structured 12-month rotations.
  • Strategic Contribution: Analyse complex situations and propose purposeful solutions aligned with Shell's Net-Zero goals.
  • Mentorship: Receive personalised coaching from senior leaders and industry experts.
  • Innovation: Embrace a learner mindset to navigate adaptability in changing global environments.
  • Honesty & Integrity: Work in a culture where ethics and respect are core values.

Market Trend: Digital Transformation in Energy

The energy sector is no longer just about oil and gas. By 2030, the global Renewables and Power market is expected to dominate investment. Shell's "Powering Progress" strategy focuses on digitalization and R&D to lead this transition. Graduates in the 2026 cohort will be at the forefront of Digital Twins, Predictive Maintenance, and AI-driven energy optimization—skills that are now the gold standard in global engineering.

Detailed Selection Process

1. Online Cognitive Assessment

Initial screening evaluating your capacity to absorb information and solve numerical/verbal reasoning problems.

2. On-Demand Video Interview

A structured recording where you share your unique perspective and achievements. Focus on the "Relationship" criteria here.

3. Shell Recruitment Day (Final Round)

A deep-dive virtual assessment day involving case studies and technical/behavioral interviews with senior leaders.

Eligibility & Technical Foundation

Bachelors / Masters / PhD Less than 3 Yrs Experience Strong Communication India Work Auth Honesty & Respect
  • Education: Graduates prior to their first day at Shell. All streams eligible.
  • Experience: Must have less than three years of professional experience post-graduation.
  • Soft Skills: Resilience to achieve goals and the flexibility to collaborate effectively in diverse teams.
  • Strategic Thinking: Ability to analyse information and generate original ideas.

Expert Preparation Guide: The "CAR" Model

1. Capacity (Analysing Problems)

Shell looks for individuals who can handle complexity. Practice explaining a technical problem you solved by breaking it down into simple, logical components.

2. Achievement (Drive & Resilience)

Prepare stories of when you faced a setback. How did you adapt your mindset to deliver results despite the changing environment?

3. Relationship (Collaboration)

Shell values "Inclusion." Be ready to discuss how you refined an idea by being open to alternative perspectives from team members.

  • Case Study Practice: Review energy sector case studies—focus on Hydrogen, Solar, and Integrated Gas.
