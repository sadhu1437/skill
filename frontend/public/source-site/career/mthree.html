What is the mthree Graduate Program?

mthree (part of the Wiley Edge ecosystem) provides a unique "foot in the door" for ambitious graduates. The program identifies high-potential talent and provides 6-12 weeks of intense, industry-expert-led training at their Academy. Post-training, you are placed as an mthree Alumni with prestigious clients—ranging from top-tier investment banks to global leaders in healthcare, aviation, and insurance.

Starting CTC ₹9.15 LPA

Eligibility CS / STEM Degree

Comprehensive 8-Stage Selection Journey

mthree follows a meritocratic process to ensure only the most dedicated candidates enter the Academy:

Stage 1-2: Application & Online Assessment

Apply with your latest CV. Shortlisted candidates face an Aptitude and Coding assessment focusing on logical reasoning and core programming logic.

Stage 3-5: Screenings & Interviews

Includes a Communication Screening, a rigorous Technical Interview (OOPs, DS, SDLC), and a Final Interview with mthree leadership.

Stage 6-8: Academy & Placement

Join the 6-12 week training. Upon passing the end-of-training assessment, you are selected for placement in a niche job role that matches your competence.

Market Trend: Technology in Investment Banking

The financial sector is currently undergoing a massive digital overhaul. Investment banks are heavily investing in Algorithmic Trading Engines, Low-Latency Systems, and Regulatory Reporting Platforms. Joining mthree puts you at the intersection of Finance and Technology—one of the most high-paying and stable career paths globally.

Requirements & Skill Set

Java / Python / C++ OOPs & Design Patterns Multithreading SDLC & TDD Data Structures
  • Academic Stand: CS, Tech, or related STEM degree with 60% aggregate and no standing arrears.
  • Coding Proficiency: Strong command over Java, Python, or C/C++. Competitive coding experience is a major plus.
  • Engineering Principles: Solid understanding of multithreading, memory management, and design patterns.
  • Methodologies: Familiarity with Test-Driven Development (TDD) and the formal SDLC.

Expert Preparation Roadmap for mthree

1. Core Technical Foundations

mthree's clients (Banks) value Core Java and Python. Focus on Garbage Collection, Exception Handling, and Collections Framework. Understand how to design stable, scalable applications.

2. Troubleshoot & Debug

The technical interview often includes "Debug this code" scenarios. Practice identifying logical errors and edge cases in your code on platforms like LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks.

3. Communication is Key

Since you will be working with global teams, the Communication Screening is vital. Practice explaining your projects clearly and concisely using the STAR method.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Finance Basics: Research basic banking terms like Equity, Fixed Income, and Compliance. Showing interest in the banking domain will set you apart.
  • GitHub Review: Clean up your GitHub. Ensure you have a project that demonstrates your understanding of Design Patterns or Multithreading.
  • Certifications: Pursue an Oracle Java Certification or a Python for Data Science badge to validate your technical claims.
