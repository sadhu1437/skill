High-Growth Tech Role

Associate DevOps Support Engineer

Copado - The #1 DevOps Platform for Salesforce and the Cloud

Experience: 0-2 Years | Industry: SaaS & Cloud Enterprise

Drive Overview

Copado is revolutionizing the way enterprises develop and release software. We deliver an AI-enabled DevOps platform that unites non-technical admins and pro-code developers to orchestrate business application development. Driving digital transformation for 1,500+ of the most innovative brands on the planet—including Coca-Cola, T-Mobile, and Volkswagen—we empower enterprises to scale end-to-end software delivery across multi-cloud environments. We are looking for an ambitious Associate DevOps Support Engineer to serve as the front line of technical problem-solving for our global customers.

Target Audience Freshers / 0-2 Yrs
Required Degree B.Tech / B.E (CS/IT)
Core Domain Salesforce & DevOps

Your Role & Responsibilities

In this dynamic, high-touch role, you are the ultimate traffic controller and problem-solver for a fast-paced DevOps environment. Speed and accuracy are your main assets.

  • Rapid Response Triage: Answer incoming support queries with a strict goal to get the customer connected to a human within a 10-minute SLA.
  • Voice Calls & Traffic: Serve as the front line for inbound customer voice calls, directing traffic and managing queues efficiently.
  • Troubleshoot & Resolve: Actively diagnose technical problems, answer questions, and execute quick fixes related to the Copado platform independently.
  • Escalate Accurately: Identify, categorize, and seamlessly route tougher technical issues to Tier 2/3 internal experts while following up relentlessly.
  • Own the Customer Experience: Communicate effectively with users to manage expectations, resulting in high Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores.

Market Trend: Salesforce DevOps & Agentic AI

The Salesforce ecosystem is massive, and managing software releases within it has traditionally been complex. Copado leads the market by providing CI/CD (Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment) specifically tailored for Salesforce. In the new "Agentic Age," Copado uses AI agents to automate software delivery. Joining as an Associate DevOps Support Engineer immerses you in Cloud APIs, Git version control, and SaaS architecture, establishing a high-value career pathway into Cloud DevOps Engineering and Salesforce Architecture.

Eligibility & Technical Requirements

B.Tech CS/IT Git / Version Control APIs & XML SQL Salesforce Basics
  • Education: B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a closely related engineering discipline.
  • Experience: Freshers are welcome! Candidates with 0–2 years in Tech Support, DevOps Support, or the Salesforce ecosystem (Admin/QA) are highly eligible.
  • Core Tech: Familiarity with SaaS applications, APIs, XML, SQL, Git, and version control systems.
  • Salesforce: Basic understanding of Salesforce platforms and general DevOps concepts (CI/CD).
  • Soft Skills: Excellent written and verbal English communication skills. Ability to multitask by researching solutions while maintaining customer communication.

Expert Preparation Guide for Copado

1. Understand DevOps & Version Control

You must understand what Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) mean. Brush up on Git commands (commit, push, pull, merge conflicts) as Copado's core functionality revolves around deploying code cleanly across environments.

2. Salesforce Ecosystem Basics

You don't need to be an expert developer, but you should know what a "Sandbox," "Production environment," and "Metadata" mean in the context of Salesforce. Reviewing the Trailhead modules for "Salesforce Admin Basics" will give you a massive advantage.

3. Mastering SLA and Triage Metrics

The JD specifically states a "10-minute SLA" for connecting human-to-human. In your interview, demonstrate extreme urgency and prioritization skills. Use the STAR method to explain how you handle high-pressure situations or angry clients while maintaining a calm, empathetic tone.
