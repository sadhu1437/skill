100% Sponsored M.Tech

Junior Technical Trainer

iamneo (An NIIT Venture) - Closing the Tech Skills Gap Through AI-Driven Learning

Location: PAN India (Onboarding in Coimbatore) | Work-Integrated Learning Program (WILP)

Drive Overview

Most fresher jobs ask you to pick a lane: work, or study further. This one doesn't make you choose. Founded in 2016 and now part of the NIIT ecosystem, iamneo is a fast-growing, profitable B2B EdTech SaaS company. We work with enterprise leaders across ITeS, BFSI, and Higher Education to power cloud-based Full Stack Labs and auto-evaluation tools. We are looking for fresh 2025 & 2026 B.Tech/B.E. graduates who love explaining concepts, debugging code, and helping others succeed to join our Work-Integrated Learning Program (WILP).

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Grads
Initial Salary ₹3.5 LPA (Years 1-2)
Post-WILP Salary ₹6.0 LPA (Year 3+)

What's In It For You?

This is a full-time job from day one. Not an internship. Not a trainee tag. A real role, real responsibility, real learners.

  • 100% Sponsored M.Tech: Earn a postgraduate degree while you work — fully funded, zero cost to you.
  • Learn by Teaching: Nothing sharpens your own coding skills faster than debugging someone else's. You'll grow technically just by doing this job well.
  • Structured Growth Path: A clear track into Technical Training, L&D, and EdTech product/innovation roles.
  • Work With Industry Leaders: Empower learners across organizations like Wipro, HCLTech, LTIMindtree, and premier educational institutions.

Your Role & Responsibilities

Here's what you'll actually be doing:

  • Deliver live technical training sessions and run hands-on coding labs.
  • Mentor learners and unblock them in real time when their code (or their confidence) breaks.
  • Evaluate assignments, give constructive feedback, and track learner progress.
  • Debug alongside learners to strengthen their conceptual understanding — not just their syntax.
  • Develop learning content: detailed course materials, exercises, cheat sheets, assessments, and quick-reference guides.
  • Travel to training deployments across locations as needed.

Eligibility & Skills

B.E / B.Tech CS / IT / ECE Java / Python / JS Excellent English Mentoring
  • Education: B.Tech / B.E. graduates (CS, IT, ECE, or related branches).
  • Graduation Year: Strictly 2025 or 2026 pass-outs.
  • Programming: Solid working knowledge of at least one programming language (Java, Python, JavaScript, C++, etc.).
  • Communication: Confident, clear communication in English. You'll be explaining concepts out loud every day. (Hindi is a bonus).
  • Mindset: Genuinely enjoy mentoring and want to see other people succeed.
  • Commitment: Ready to commit 3 years to the WILP program.

The Rigorous Selection Process

We want trainers who are genuinely strong at both code and communication. Strong English communication is evaluated at every single step.

1. Aptitude Assessment

Screens candidates for problem-solving fundamentals before they're invited into the Pre-Hiring Learning Program.

2. Pre-Hiring Learning Program (10–12 days)

Before any interview, you'll get self-paced access to a learning track (e.g., DSA-C++ or JAVA). Learn the stack, then take a final assessment. This decides if you move forward.

3. Live Coding Assessment & Presentation

Can you actually code and think out loud under real conditions? You will need to explain your thinking clearly while coding.

4. Psychometric Assessment

Evaluates your personality and work-style fit for a 3-year training commitment and a mentoring-heavy role.

5. HR Culture Fit

Final check on your commitment to the WILP journey, cultural alignment, and communication skills.

Expert Preparation Guide

  • Practice "Thinking Aloud": When solving coding problems on LeetCode or HackerRank, practice explaining every step of your logic out loud, as if teaching a beginner.
  • Master the Fundamentals: Your core language (Java, Python, or C++) must be rock solid. You cannot debug someone else's code if you don't understand the underlying concepts (OOPs, memory management, basic data structures).
  • Embrace the Pre-Hiring Program: Treat the 10-12 day learning program seriously. It's designed to set you up for success in the live coding round.
