IT Support Engineer Intern

Zycus - Powering Source-to-Pay Procurement with Merlin AI

Location: Bangalore, India | Work Model: Hybrid

Drive Overview

Zycus is a global leader in Source-to-Pay (S2P) procurement software, trusted by Fortune 1000 organizations. At the core of our platform is Merlin AI, an advanced engine that brings automation and predictive insights across the entire procurement lifecycle. We are looking for enthusiastic and motivated fresh graduates to join our IT Support team as IT Support Engineer Interns. If you are interested in enterprise application support and troubleshooting, this 6-month program in Bangalore will accelerate your career in IT operations.

Role Internship (6 Months)
Target Degree B.E / B.Tech (CS/IT/EC)
Target Batch 2025 & Beyond

Your Role & Responsibilities

You will work closely with senior support engineers to monitor applications, resolve user issues, and support the day-to-day operations of advanced Zycus platforms.

  • L1 Support: Provide first-level support for Zycus applications and related enterprise systems.
  • Troubleshooting: Resolve basic application, user access, and system-related issues for global clients.
  • Incident Tracking: Monitor support tickets, coordinate with internal teams and business users, and provide regular updates.
  • Process Learning: Learn and actively support Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Source-to-Pay (S2P) operational workflows.
  • Validation & Documentation: Assist in application testing, validation, and maintaining accurate knowledge articles and reports.
  • SLA Compliance: Strictly follow IT support processes, SLAs, and escalation procedures to ensure minimal downtime.

Eligibility & Skill Requirements

Opportunity Note: This is a 6-month internship in Bangalore on a hybrid model. High-performing interns often get prioritized for full-time enterprise support roles.
B.E / B.Tech 2025 Batch+ Ticketing Tools Troubleshooting App Support
  • Education: BE/B.Tech is mandatory (Computer Science, IT, Electronics, or related streams preferred).
  • Graduation Year: Eligible candidates must be 2025 graduates or later.
  • Technical Foundation: Basic understanding of application support concepts and system monitoring.
  • Problem Solving: Good analytical and logical troubleshooting skills.
  • Interpersonal Skills: Strong communication skills and a willingness to learn enterprise applications.
  • Bonus Skills: Basic knowledge of ticketing tools (Jira, Zendesk, ServiceNow) or IT support processes (ITIL) is a huge advantage.

Expert Preparation Guide for Application Support

1. ITSM & Ticketing Basics

Zycus values engineers who understand support frameworks. Familiarize yourself with ITIL terms like Incident vs. Request, SLAs, and Escalation Matrix. Mentioning tools like Jira or ServiceNow will make your profile stand out.

2. Domain Knowledge (P2P / S2P)

The job description mentions supporting Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Source-to-Pay (S2P) workflows. Read a quick 10-minute article on how these business processes work. Understanding the business logic makes troubleshooting app errors much easier.

3. The Troubleshooting Mindset

In interviews, you might be asked situational questions (e.g., "A user cannot log in, what are your first three steps?"). Practice a structured approach: Check credentials, check network, check browser cache, and verify system logs.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Research Merlin AI: Zycus’s core platform uses AI. Briefly read about how AI is transforming procurement to show genuine interest in the company's product during HR rounds.
  • Learn API Basics: In modern enterprise apps, knowing how to test an API endpoint (using Postman or browser DevTools) is an incredible asset for L1 support.
