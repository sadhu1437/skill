Associate Analyst (Non-Voice)

GlobalLogic - A Hitachi Group Company | Global Maps & Tech Research

Location: Hyderabad & Mahabubnagar | Function: Science Technical Research

Drive Overview

GlobalLogic, a Hitachi Group Company, is a leader in digital engineering helping brands across the globe design and build innovative products. We are seeking highly detailed and analytical individuals to join our team as a Global Maps Data Associate Analyst. In this non-voice, operational role, you will perform deep-dive analysis on map defects and routing opportunities. This is the perfect entry-level opportunity for fresh graduates to enter the high-growth geospatial and data analytics space.

Experience Level 0 - 1 Years (Fresher)
Target Degree Any Graduation
Work Model On-site (Rotational)

What You Will Do

You will act as the human intelligence layer ensuring the accuracy of ETA, traffic, routing, and places across the global maps ecosystem.

Data Analysis & Investigation

Analyze large, complex datasets to identify anomalies, unexpected behaviors, and region-specific defects across the global maps ecosystem. Investigate issues raised by product and engineering teams and deliver detailed, actionable analysis.

Insights & Reporting

Identify high-impact opportunities from raw data and categorize them into meaningful map-level insights. Provide weekly insights and trends to leadership, backed by robust data validation.

Process & Quality Execution

Perform repetitive data processing and quality checks at high speed and accuracy levels. Maintain strict adherence to client and company policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and quality guidelines within defined SLAs.

Market Trend: Geospatial Data & AI Intelligence

Modern applications—from ride-sharing apps to autonomous vehicles—rely entirely on flawless map data. The "Science Technical Research" division handles complex routing, ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival), and traffic anomalies. As an Associate Analyst here, you are directly training and correcting the algorithms that power global navigation systems. This exposure builds a strong foundation for future roles in Data Quality Assurance, Operations Analytics, and AI Data Labeling.

Eligibility & Skill Requirements

Mandatory Document Rule: Candidates MUST possess their Provisional Certificate and Consolidated Marks Memo (CMM) from a recognized institution. Immediate joiners only.
Data Analysis Skills Anomaly Detection SLA Management Attention to Detail Non-Voice Ops
  • Education: A graduation degree from a recognized institution.
  • Experience: 0 to 1 years of experience (Freshers are highly encouraged).
  • Analytical: Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to identify anomalies and detect meaningful patterns in large datasets.
  • Comprehension: Proficiency in understanding, interpreting, and applying complex policies and standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • Flexibility: Mandatory willingness to work from the office in rotational shifts (5 working days, 2 weekly offs).

Expert Preparation Guide for Maps Analyst Role

1. Attention to Detail & Pattern Recognition

The core of this job is "repetitive data processing at high speed." Practice your focus. Interviewers might give you a visual or data-based test to spot errors or anomalies in a map layout or a dataset table.

2. SOP & Policy Adherence

You will be handling complex guidelines. Be ready to explain how you follow instructions meticulously. Use the STAR method to describe a past scenario where following strict rules led to a successful outcome.

3. Behavioral Readiness (Rotational Shifts)

GlobalLogic explicitly mentions a 24/7 environment and rotational shifts. Be prepared to assure the HR team that you are adaptable, independent, and capable of managing your productivity during non-standard hours.
