Retail Tech Powerhouse

Apprentice - Technology

Target Corporation - Expect More. Pay Less.

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka | Job ID: R0000348368

Drive Overview

As a Fortune 50 company with more than 400,000 team members worldwide, Target is an iconic brand and one of America's leading retailers. Target Technology Services is the behind-the-scenes powerhouse fueling innovation. We rely on the brightest people to deliver incredible value to guests online and in stores. In this Technology Apprentice role, you will join our fully integrated Bengaluru hub of over 4,000 team members to learn, design, and automate industry-leading retail technologies.

Target Audience Engineering Students
Eligible Branches CS / IT / Circuit
Job Type Internship / Full-time

What You Will Do

This apprenticeship is a hands-on learning experience. You won't just shadow; you will actively contribute to Target’s Intellectual Property and retail algorithms.

  • Software Engineering: Learn and work alongside seasoned engineering professionals to design and build scalable, secure deliverables.
  • Automation & Debugging: Participate in structured construction, automation, debugging, and implementation activities.
  • Tech Evaluation: Use your technology acumen to provide input on and evaluate new, emerging technologies.
  • Issue Resolution: Resolve frequently encountered technical issues and monitor systems capacity with minimal assistance.
  • Lifecycle Management: Contribute to the design, lifecycle management, and total cost of ownership of retail services.

Eligibility & Mindset Requirements

B.E / B.Tech Computer Science / IT Circuit Branches (ECE/EEE) No Active Backlogs Problem Solving
  • Education: Pursuing an Engineering degree with a Computer Science, Information Technology, or Circuit Branch specialization.
  • Academics: Must have a strong CGPA or Aggregate as prescribed by the university with no active backlogs.
  • Tech Passion: A strong, demonstrable passion for learning new tools, automation scripts, and development technologies.
  • Soft Skills: Team player mindset with a "can-do" attitude, authenticity, and respect for diversity and inclusion.
  • Execution: Ability to follow through with ideas and deliver on commitments in an agile environment.

Expert Preparation Guide for Target

1. Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)

Target values solid computer science fundamentals. Brush up on your logic for Arrays, Strings, Trees, and Maps. Be ready to write clean, optimized code on a whiteboard or virtual IDE during technical rounds.

2. The Retail Tech Landscape

Understanding e-Commerce challenges is a huge plus. Read about how large retailers manage inventory databases, handle peak sale traffic (like Black Friday), and secure customer data. Discussing "Scalability" in interviews will set you apart.

3. Cultural Fit & Authenticity

Target explicitly states that inclusion is part of their core value. Be authentic during HR rounds. Use the STAR method to explain a time you collaborated with a diverse group or learned a new technology quickly.

What You Will Gain

  • A hands-on overview of the fast-paced retail and e-Commerce business landscape.
  • Proficiency in the latest enterprise tools and development technologies.
  • The opportunity to interact with and learn from global teams and leaders.
  • Association with a growing, high-performing group of technologists, Engineers, and Data Scientists.
