Drive Overview

We're an international bank, nimble enough to act, big enough for impact. For more than 160 years, Standard Chartered has worked to make a positive difference. Our Technology & Operations (T&O) team is the powerhouse for the Bank, ensuring we're agile and ready for tomorrow, today. We are looking for curious technologists from the 2025 and 2026 batches to join our 12-Month Apprenticeship Programme in Bangalore. You will collaborate with driven technologists to build banking solutions that provide the best digital experiences globally.

Primary Stack J2EE, Spring Boot, APIs

The Apprenticeship Experience

This programme gives you the opportunity to gain first-hand experience working on real-world problems, hone your skills, create lasting relationships, and discover your strengths.

  • Real-World Impact: Tackle real-life business problem-solving by enhancing existing offerings and innovating new digital experiences.
  • Comprehensive Training: Receive technical and professional skills training, targeted online learning, and future skills development workshops.
  • Agile Delivery: Understand and participate in the formal Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) and Agile Delivery Approach.
  • Mentorship: Attend inspiring thought-leadership speaker events and collaborate with highly driven technologists.

Eligibility & Technical Requirements

Java / J2EE Spring Boot / Hibernate REST / SOAP / JSON Linux / UNIX Oracle / PostgreSQL
  • Education: Final year students (2026 batch) or 2025 passed-outs from Engineering degrees with excellent academic records.
  • Availability: Able to start the apprenticeship in August 2026.
  • Web Applications: Experience in project initiatives using J2EE-based web applications (Spring Boot, JPA, JSP, Servlet, JDBC, Hibernate) and Web security.
  • Web Services: Knowledge of Web Services standards and technologies including SOA, XML, SOAP, WSDL, REST, JSON, and JSON API.
  • Systems & Databases: Solid foundational knowledge of Linux/UNIX commands and databases (Oracle, PostgreSQL).
  • Mindset: Agile, flexible team players open to learning, with strong technical abilities and extracurricular activities.

Expert Preparation Guide for Standard Chartered

1. Deep Dive into J2EE & Spring Boot

The job description heavily emphasizes the Java ecosystem. Ensure you understand core Java, OOPs, and the Spring Boot framework. Be prepared to explain how Dependency Injection, JPA/Hibernate, and Servlets work under the hood in an enterprise application.

2. Mastering Web Services (REST vs. SOAP)

In the banking sector, both legacy and modern systems coexist. You must know the difference between REST and SOAP APIs. Understand concepts like WSDL, XML parsing, JSON structures, and how to secure a web API against vulnerabilities.

3. Databases & UNIX Fundamentals

Expect questions on relational databases. Practice writing SQL queries for Oracle or PostgreSQL (joins, indexing, transactions). Additionally, basic Linux/UNIX commands (like grep, awk, file permissions) are essential for backend troubleshooting.

4. Cultural Alignment & Assessments

Standard Chartered values diversity, inclusion, and a "never settle" attitude. Be ready for situational judgment tests and technical assessments prior to interviews. Use the STAR method to highlight your agility and teamwork in past projects.
