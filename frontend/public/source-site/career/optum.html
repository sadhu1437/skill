Software Engineer (AI/ML & GenAI)

Optum (UnitedHealth Group) - Caring. Connecting. Growing Together.

Requisition: 2359819 | Hyderabad, Telangana | Tech Hub

Drive Overview

Optum is a global health services innovation company, part of UnitedHealth Group (Fortune 5). We are on a mission to advance health optimization on a global scale. We are currently seeking a Software Engineer specializing in AI/ML and Generative AI for our Hyderabad center. This role is a core part of our tech transformation, where you will build intelligent applications that directly improve health outcomes and connect patients to the care they need.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Grad
Qualification B.Tech (CS/IT)
Focus Area GenAI & LLMs

What You Will Do

You will be mentored by senior architects to build scalable, reliable AI systems for the world's largest healthcare ecosystem.

  • GenAI Development: Design and deploy solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI frameworks.
  • Prompt Engineering: Support model evaluation and fine-tuning to improve output safety and clinical accuracy.
  • Data Science Pipelines: Participate in data collection, cleaning, and feature engineering for healthcare datasets.
  • System Integration: Implement AI models using APIs, SDKs, and frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
  • Responsible AI: Follow best practices for AI fairness, privacy, and bias monitoring.
  • Agile Execution: Work in cross-functional Agile squads (Product, Data Science, Engineering) to ship code iteratively.

Market Trend: Generative AI in Healthcare

The Healthcare AI market is expected to reach $187 Billion by 2030. Companies like Optum are moving beyond predictive models to Generative clinical summaries and Agentic care assistants. Joining this team in 2026 positions you at the forefront of "Bio-Technical Convergence," where software directly saves lives through automated diagnostics and intelligent resource allocation. Mastery of LLM orchestration is currently the highest-paid skill in the global IT sector.

Optum Selection Process Roadmap

1. Cognitive & Technical Assessment

Online test covering Logical Reasoning, Data Structures, and basic Machine Learning theory.

2. Technical Interview (AI Focus)

Deep dive into your project work, OOPS concepts, and your understanding of Python/LLM fundamentals.

3. Managerial & Value Fit Round

Discussion on Optum’s values: Integrity, Compassion, Innovation, and Performance.

Expert Preparation Guide for AI Engineer

1. LLM Foundations

Be ready to explain the difference between Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and fine-tuning. Optum values engineers who understand how to minimize hallucinations in healthcare data.

2. Prompt Engineering Skills

Practice writing structured prompts. Understand few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought, and how to evaluate model relevance for clinical use cases.

3. Responsible AI Knowledge

Research "AI Fairness" and "Privacy-Preserving ML." In healthcare, understanding how to protect patient data (HIPAA context) while training models is a massive differentiator.
