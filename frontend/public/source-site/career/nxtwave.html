Business Development Associate (Sales)

NxtWave - Revolutionizing Tech Education via Vernacular Learning

Work From Home | Native Speakers: Telugu, Tamil, Hindi

Drive Overview

NxtWave is one of India’s fastest-growing Ed-Tech startups, backed by a massive ₹275 crore funding led by Greater Pacific Capital. Founded by alumni from Amazon, IIT, and IIIT Hyderabad, NxtWave is on a mission to transform youth into highly skilled tech professionals regardless of their background. With their unique CCBP 4.0 programs, they have already helped learners get hired by over 2500+ companies including Google, Amazon, and Nvidia. We are looking for dedicated individuals to join our sales force and act as career mentors for the next generation of engineers.

Total CTC ₹6.00 LPA
Work Model WFH (Currently)
Fixed Component ₹3.60 LPA

What will you be doing?

The BDA role at NxtWave is a hybrid of "Career Counselling" and "High-Performance Sales." You are the source of advice for potential learners.

  • Mentorship & Guidance: Act as a source of career advice, helping prospects understand how CCBP 4.0 can accelerate their tech careers.
  • Sales Lifecycle: Take full responsibility for the sales closing cycle—from phone/video calls to product demonstrations and final enrollment.
  • Database Management: Maintain detailed interactions in the CRM and provide feedback on lead quality.
  • Target Achievement: Carry weekly revenue and enrollment targets to drive NxtWave's mission across 647+ districts.
  • Rapport Building: Use empathy and patience to build trust with prospects and their families.

Market Trend: The Vernacular EdTech Revolution

India's EdTech market is projected to reach $10 Billion by 2026. The biggest barrier to entry for high-paying tech jobs has historically been the English language. NxtWave is disrupting this by offering content in Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi. As a BDA, you are selling more than a course; you are selling "Outcome-driven Education" in a language the learner understands best. This localized approach results in 3x higher retention and significantly better job outcomes for students from tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

Detailed Selection Process

Step 1: Application Screening

Shortlisting based on your native language proficiency (Telugu/Tamil/Hindi) and your interest in the EdTech domain.

Step 2: Business Communication Round

A telephonic or video screening to check your fluency, tonality, and ability to handle basic objections in your native language.

Step 3: Sales Pitch / Mock Demo

You will be asked to simulate a sales call. The panel will evaluate your empathy, systematic approach to building a funnel, and closing skills.

Step 4: Final HR Interview

Discussion regarding your determination, flexibility for a 6-day week, and alignment with NxtWave’s high-growth culture.

Expert Preparation Guide: The "Outcome Pitch"

1. Empathy over Selling

NxtWave focuses on Outcome-driven education. Don't just talk about the syllabus; talk about the 2500+ companies hiring their students. Your pitch should focus on the "Career Transformation."

2. Handling Parent Objections

In the EdTech B2C model, you often talk to parents. Practice explaining the value of tech skills and job security in a way that someone from a non-tech background can understand.

3. Master the CCBP 4.0 USP

Research why CCBP 4.0 is unique (NSDC partnership, vernacular content, 4.0 technologies). Being able to articulate the "Uniqueness" is a key JD requirement.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Watch Impact Stories: Visit NxtWave’s LinkedIn or YouTube and watch learner impact stories. This will give you real-life examples to use in your mock pitch.
  • Practice Tonality: Record yourself explaining a complex tech concept in your native language. Aim for a "Mentor-like" authoritative yet friendly tone.
  • CRM Basics: Familiarize yourself with how sales funnels work. Knowing how to prioritize "Hot Leads" will impress the interviewers.
