Purplle Tech Challenge 2026

Building "Unbreakable Systems" for 60M+ Users

HackerEarth Hackathon | May 22 - May 24, 2026

Challenge Overview

At Purplle.com, we’re looking for builders. The kind who don’t just solve problems — they rethink them. The Purplle Tech Challenge isn’t just a coding test; it’s an opportunity to tackle real problems pulled directly from how 60 million+ users experience beauty every day. We are moving towards "Systematic Resilience," transitioning away from manual firefighting to an era where AI builds self-healing infrastructure. If you want to stop "fixing" and start "architecting" at the highest level, this challenge is your entry point.

Event Dates May 22 - 24, 2026
Target Audience Exp. Pros & Interns
Event Type Online Hackathon

Who Should Apply?

This is an individual hiring challenge open to both experienced professionals and 3rd/4th-year engineering students across multiple tracks.

  • Software Engineers (SDE II): 1–6 years of experience.
  • Data Scientists (DS I & II): 1+ years of experience.
  • Data Engineers: 1+ years of experience.
  • Frontend & Backend Engineers: 1+ years of experience.
  • Engineering Interns: Currently in 3rd or 4th year of study.

The Purplle Way: AI-Native Engineering

We believe that as a business scales, technology shouldn’t become more fragile. Purplle is embedding AI across its entire engineering lifecycle:

  • Velocity without Fear: We aim for 70% of Pull Requests to be AI-assisted, allowing you to focus on high-level logic rather than boilerplate code.
  • Self-Healing Systems: We build "Golden Paths" that use AI for automated rollbacks and deterministic fallback logic.
  • Zero-Toil Operations: Utilizing AI agents to eliminate manual operations toil, diagnosing anomalies in minutes.
  • Predictable Economics: Optimizing cloud spend and latency as core engineering disciplines.

Challenge Structure & Roadmap

Round 1 (R1): The Weekend Challenge

Take on the 2-hour online challenge between May 22 and May 24. You will attempt only 1 out of 4 coding problems. Choose the one that best showcases your algorithmic optimization.

Round 2 (R2): Advanced Evaluation

Top builders from R1 move forward. This round includes deeper technical evaluations and system design discussions with Purplle’s tech team.

The Fast-Track: Final Interviews

Finalists get fast-tracked directly to interviews with Purplle’s tech leadership, bypassing traditional screening queues.

Expert Preparation Guide for Purplle

1. Adopt "The Systems Thinker" Mindset

Purplle evaluates code not just for output, but for resilience. When solving the R1 challenge, consider edge cases that might cause a system to crash and handle them gracefully. They aim for a Change Failure Rate of <1.5%.

2. Demonstrate AI-Fluency

Since Purplle is an AI-first engineering team, showing familiarity with how to optimize code using AI assistants (like Copilot) or how to design systems that self-heal will be a massive advantage in R2 discussions.

3. Economic Discipline in Code

Consider the Time and Space Complexity of your algorithm. Purplle cares about "unit economics"—meaning your code should scale efficiently without massively increasing cloud costs.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Practice Platform: Familiarize yourself with the HackerEarth coding environment beforehand to avoid UI confusion during the 2-hour window.
  • Product Thinking: Think about how a beauty e-commerce platform handles 60M users. Consider caching strategies or recommendation engine basics.
  • Read the Guidelines: Remember, attempting multiple questions will disqualify the subsequent ones. Focus entirely on optimizing one solution.
  • SkillBloom Resource: Download our "HackerEarth Hackathon Strategy Guide" from the PDF section for time-management tips during R1.
