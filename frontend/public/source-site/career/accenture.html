Packaged App Development Associate

Accenture - Let there be change

PAN India Hubs | 24x7 Shifts | Full-Time Role

Drive Overview

Accenture is a leading global professional services company that helps the world's leading businesses, governments, and organizations build their digital core. With approximately 799,000 people serving clients in over 120 countries, Accenture is driving change through cloud, data, and AI. We are currently seeking Associate Software Engineers (ASE) / Packaged App Development Associates to design, build, and support technology solutions that meet client requirements from analysis to implementation.

Target Batch Till 2024 Passouts
Experience Level 0 - 23 Months Max
Job Locations PAN India

What You Will Do

You will work in challenging and dynamic environments, using your versatility to support applications across various technology stacks.

  • End-to-End Development: Design, build, test, assemble, and support applications based on business requirements.
  • Tech Modernization: Bake technology trends into solutions and integrate new functionality into existing systems.
  • Testing & QA: Create test designs, test cases, and test data. Analyze results and submit observations to development teams.
  • Incident Management: Resolve incidents and extend support to customers through voice, email, chat, or remote support.
  • Infrastructure: Support infrastructure operations and manage delivery for IT production systems.

Preferred Technical Skills

J2EE / Full-Stack .NET (ASP.NET MVC) ABAP on HANA Salesforce Test Automation

While this is an entry-level position, having a working knowledge of any of the above tracks will give you a significant advantage during the technical evaluation phases.

Strict Eligibility Criteria

  • Degree: B.E/B.Tech/M.E/MTech or MCA or MSc (CS/IT/Computer Applications/Data Science).
  • Batch: Any year of pass out till 2024.
  • Experience Cap: Candidates must not have more than 23 months of full-time experience.
  • Academic Gap: There should be no gaps during the course of your degree (e.g., a 4-year B.Tech must be completed in exactly 4 years).
  • Backlogs: No active backlogs during the application or onboarding phase.
  • Cooling Period: You should not have appeared for an Accenture assessment/interview in the last 3 months.
  • Citizenship: Indian citizenship (or Nepal/Bhutan nationals; others require an OCI/PIO card).
⚠️ Accenture Fraud Alert: Accenture’s criterion for hiring is purely based on merit. Accenture has not authorized any agency, company, or individual to collect money in exchange for a job. If approached by anyone demanding money for an Accenture offer, please report it to businessethicsline.com/accenture.

The Accenture Selection Roadmap

Accenture's hiring process is unique and strictly timed. Here is what you need to prepare for:

1. Cognitive & Technical Assessment

A 90-minute test comprising English Ability, Critical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, Pseudo Coding, Network Security, and Cloud basics. You must clear the Cognitive section to proceed to the Technical section.

2. Coding Assessment

A 45-minute round with 2 coding questions. You can choose to code in C, C++, Dot Net, Java, or Python. Focus on arrays, strings, and basic data structures.

3. Communication Assessment

A non-technical, AI-driven vocal test lasting ~20 minutes. It evaluates Sentence Mastery, Vocabulary, Fluency, and Pronunciation. Ensure you take this in a completely quiet room.

4. Final Interview

A combined Technical and HR round. Expect questions on your final year project, OOPS concepts, and situational questions related to adapting to new technologies.
