Service Desk Role

Cognizant - Digital Workplace Practice | Transforming Experiences

Exclusive Drive for 2025 & 2026 3-Year UG Degree Graduates

Drive Overview

Cognizant engineers modern businesses. We help clients modernize technology, reimagine processes, and transform experiences so they can stay ahead in our fast-changing world. We are currently launching our campus hiring drive for the 2025 and 2026 batches to join our Digital Workplace Practice. In this Service Desk Role, you will act as the first point of contact for global enterprise clients, solving critical IT issues and ensuring smooth business operations.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Grads
Degree Required 3-Year UG Only
Job Location PAN India

What You Will Do

You will be the face of IT for our clients, requiring a client-focused mindset and the ability to support users across diverse cultures.

  • Primary Support: Serve as the first point of contact in the Service Desk for end-users reporting IT issues.
  • Issue Resolution: Log, track, and resolve Service Desk tickets accurately and within defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Troubleshooting: Identify and troubleshoot hardware and software issues to minimize downtime and disruption.
  • Escalation Management: Collaborate with internal IT teams (L2/L3 support) to escalate and resolve complex technical scenarios.
  • Documentation: Build and maintain knowledge base articles to enable faster future resolutions.
  • Shifts: Demonstrate a willingness to work in shifts (including night shifts) directly from the office.

Cognizant invests heavily in its early-career professionals. This role comes with exceptional benefits designed for 3-year graduates:

  • Higher Education Cognizant Sponsored Higher Education from BITS Pilani (Applicable only to candidates from Science, Technology & Mathematics backgrounds).
  • No Bonds Absolutely No Service bonds or financial lock-ins.
  • Allowances Generous Night shift allowance for non-standard hours.
  • Transport Safe and comfortable cab facilities for pick-up and drop.
  • Growth Timely rewards, recognition, and clear progression paths for top performers.

Eligibility Criteria (Strict Requirements)

B.Com / BCA / B.Sc BA / BBA / BMS Min. 50% Marks No Active Backlogs

Expert Preparation Guide for Service Desk

1. ITIL Basics & Ticketing

Familiarize yourself with ITIL frameworks (Incident Management vs. Request Fulfillment). Understand what an "SLA" (Service Level Agreement) is and why it's critical in B2B IT support.

2. Behavioral & Communication Skills

This role heavily relies on "Client-focused mindset and Communication Abilities." Practice answering situational questions using the STAR method (e.g., "How would you handle a frustrated user whose laptop crashed during a presentation?").

3. Basic IT Troubleshooting

Brush up on basic networking commands (ping, ipconfig), Active Directory concepts (password resets), and common Windows/Office 365 troubleshooting steps.
