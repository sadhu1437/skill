Jr Talent Hiring (Software Engineer)

Zensar - Shaping Experiences for Better Futures

Location: Pune, Maharashtra | RPG Group Company

Drive Overview

Zensar is an "experience-led everything" organization, conceptualizing and engineering digital solutions for over 130 leading enterprises globally. Part of the $4.8 billion RPG Group, Zensar boasts a community of 10,000+ innovators across 30+ locations. We are currently seeking highly talented Computer Science graduates from the 2025 and 2026 batches to join our Pune hub. This is an advanced engineering role focusing on algorithmic problem solving, strong data manipulation, and the rapidly growing field of Agentic AI Engineering.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Passouts
Core Skill DSA & Coding
Target Degree BE / B.Tech (CS)

Your Role & Impact

This is not a traditional support role. You will be building cutting-edge software utilizing strong coding principles and data manipulation.

  • Software Development: Write robust, scalable code utilizing sound software engineering principles.
  • Agentic Engineering: Learn and apply concepts of "Agentic AI workflows," enabling AI agents to act autonomously to solve enterprise problems.
  • Data Handling: Leverage your strong data background to manage, process, and optimize large-scale data architectures.
  • Problem Solving: Act as a primary problem solver within your team, tackling algorithmic challenges and system architecture bottlenecks.
  • Stakeholder Communication: Clearly communicate and present ideas, outcomes, and system behaviors to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Market Trend: What is Agentic Engineering?

The job description specifically mentions an understanding of Agentic Engineering. The IT industry is shifting from traditional AI (which just answers prompts) to Agentic AI—systems that can break down a goal, plan steps, use external tools (like APIs or databases), and execute tasks autonomously. Companies like Zensar are leading this shift. As a Junior Engineer equipped with DSA and Agentic skills, you will be at the forefront of the most highly paid and secure tech niche of 2026.

Required Qualifications & Skills

Data Structures Algorithms Agentic Engineering Data Background Problem Solving Communication
  • Academic Pedigree: BE / B.Tech strictly in Computer Science. Must have a strong academic pedigree (usually implying 70%+ aggregate).
  • Batch: Candidates must be from the 2025 or 2026 graduating batches.
  • Technical Depth: Exceptionally sound coding principles along with a strong foundation in Data Structures and Algorithms.
  • Innovation Mindset: Willingness to learn and adapt to new technologies (specifically in the AI/Data space).
  • Soft Skills: Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to articulate system behaviors clearly.

Expert Preparation Guide for Zensar

1. Mastery of DSA

Since "Strong Pedigree" and "DSA skills" are highlighted, expect the first round to be a heavy coding assessment. Practice medium-to-hard LeetCode problems focusing on Arrays, Trees, Dynamic Programming, and Graph theory.

2. Agentic AI & LLMs

To stand out in the interview, understand frameworks like LangChain, CrewAI, or AutoGen. Be prepared to explain how an LLM can use "Tools" or "Function Calling" to query a database autonomously.

3. Strong Data Background

Revise your RDBMS and NoSQL concepts. Understand how to process, clean, and store data efficiently, as this is a prerequisite for feeding context to AI models.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Build an AI Agent: Use Python to build a simple agentic workflow (e.g., a script that reads an email, decides if it's urgent, and adds it to a database). Host this on GitHub.
  • Competitive Coding Profile: Ensure your HackerRank or LeetCode profile links are attached to your resume to prove your "Sound Coding Principles."
  • Research Zensar's Vision: Read up on Zensar's core values: "One Zensar, Nurturing, Empowering, and Client Focus." Mentioning these during HR rounds shows deep interest.
