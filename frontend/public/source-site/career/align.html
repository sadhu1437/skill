Social Media Content Evaluator

Alignerr - Shape How AI Communicates Online

Freelance Autonomy | Flexible Hourly Contract

Drive Overview

What if your instinct for what makes a post go viral — or fall flat — could directly shape how AI communicates with millions of people online? Alignerr is looking for culturally aware individuals to review and rate AI-generated posts, captions, and comments. This is a fully remote, flexible contract role where you will help ensure AI-produced social content feels natural, engaging, and culturally on-point. No marketing degree is required—just a sharp sense of the internet landscape.

Commitment 10 – 40 Hours/Week
Job Type Hourly Contract
Experience Fresher / No Exp Req.

What You'll Do

You will work through task-based batches of content independently, on your own schedule. You are the human filter ensuring AI doesn't sound robotic or offensive.

  • Review AI Content: Evaluate AI-generated social media posts, captions, and comments across a variety of platforms and formats.
  • Assess Quality: Evaluate content for tone, relevance, engagement potential, clarity, and cultural appropriateness.
  • Flag Issues: Identify and flag content that feels unnatural, off-brand, potentially harmful, or out of step with current social trends.
  • Provide Feedback: Provide structured, actionable feedback on trending topics, cultural references, and the specific "voice" of the AI.

Market Trend: RLHF and AI Training

Why do companies pay people to read social media posts? It's called Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT or Claude learn from vast amounts of internet data, but they often struggle to understand sarcasm, modern memes, or platform-specific tones (e.g., the difference between a professional LinkedIn post and a casual TikTok caption). By evaluating this content, you are directly training the AI to be more "human" and culturally intelligent.

Who You Are (Requirements)

Active Social Media User Detail-Oriented Self-Motivated Culturally Aware
  • An active social media user with a genuine feel for what makes content resonate.
  • Culturally aware and up to date on current trends, memes, and platform norms.
  • Detail-oriented with strong judgment about tone, context, and appropriateness.
  • A clear and concise written communicator who can explain why a post works or fails.
  • Self-motivated and reliable when working independently on your own schedule.
  • No prior marketing, content creation, or AI experience required!

Expert Preparation Guide for Alignerr

1. Understand Platform Nuance

If you take an assessment, you might be asked to judge a caption. Remember: A 500-word story works on LinkedIn, but fails on TikTok. Evaluate the AI's output based on the platform it is intended for.

2. Spotting AI Hallucinations

AI often uses generic phrasing like "In today's fast-paced digital world..." or an excessive amount of emojis. Your job is to spot these unnatural patterns and flag them for improvement.

3. Cultural Safety

Be highly vigilant about content that could be misconstrued, offensive, or culturally tone-deaf. AI doesn't understand context the way humans do; your judgment is the safety net.
