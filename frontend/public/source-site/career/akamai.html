FlexBase (Remote/Hybrid)

Software Engineer

Akamai - Powering and Protecting Life Online

Location: India (Remote) | Department: Delivery Product Engineering

Drive Overview

At Akamai, we make life better for billions of people, trillions of times a day. We provide the world's most distributed platform from Cloud to Edge to help the giants of the digital world work faster and stay more secure. As a Software Engineer in the Delivery Product Engineering team, you will help large enterprises manage websites, deliver video streams, and secure transactions over the internet. You will be building high resiliency customer-facing systems operating at a scale that few others can match.

Target Audience Freshers / Jr. Engineers
Primary Stack Python, Java, APIs
Work Model 100% Remote / FlexBase

Your Role & Responsibilities

You will enhance our customers' experience in managing their international CDN presence by developing innovative solutions on the highly distributed Akamai cloud infrastructure.

  • Scalable Development: Design and develop highly scalable software that runs on the core Akamai infrastructure.
  • Collaboration: Partner with internal cross-functional teams to translate complex requirements into robust solutions.
  • Feature Implementation: Implement new features and enhance/optimize existing features for global performance.
  • Continuous Improvement: Contribute ideas and knowledge to process-improvement sessions to facilitate the continuous improvement of products.
  • Customer Focus: Build high-resiliency customer-facing systems that satisfy international compliance requirements.

Market Trend: Cloud & Edge Computing

The internet relies on speed and security. As digital consumption explodes, companies like Akamai are pioneering the shift from centralized cloud servers to Edge Computing—running apps closer to users for instant performance and neutralizing threats before they reach data centers. Working at Akamai places you at the epicenter of Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), AI scaling, and Web Security. Mastery of microservices and networking protocols (TCP/IP, HTTP) here is a massive catalyst for a long-term, high-paying tech career.

Required Skills & Qualifications

Python / Java RESTful APIs Microservices Networking (TCP/IP, DNS) Linux OS CI/CD (Jenkins)
  • Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or its equivalent.
  • Core Programming: Deep understanding of OOP concepts and proficiency in Python or Java.
  • Architecture: Familiarity with RESTful APIs, Microservices, and Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA).
  • Frameworks: Experience or familiarity with Angular, Django, Spring/SpringBoot, or Apache Airflow.
  • Databases: Exposure to relational and non-relational databases (PostgreSQL, Oracle DB, Cassandra).
  • Networking & Infra: Knowledge of Linux OS command-line tools and core networking concepts (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP).
  • DevOps: Familiarity with CI/CD tools (Jenkins, Maven) and observability tools (Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus).

Expert Preparation Guide for Akamai

1. Networking Fundamentals (Crucial)

Since Akamai powers the world's CDNs, network knowledge is highly prioritized. Be prepared to explain exactly what happens when you type a URL into a browser. Understand DNS resolution, TCP 3-way handshakes, HTTP/HTTPS protocols, and routing.

2. System Design & Microservices

Even for early-career roles, Akamai tests your understanding of scale. Brush up on how microservices communicate (REST APIs, SOA) and how to design systems that handle massive traffic using load balancers and caching.

3. Backend Frameworks (Python/Java)

Whether you choose Python (Django) or Java (Spring Boot), you must demonstrate a strong command of OOPs, exception handling, and multithreading. Expect coding rounds focused on optimizing data processing algorithms.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Understand CDN Mechanics: Read up on how Content Delivery Networks distribute traffic globally. Mentioning Akamai's edge architecture will highly impress your interviewers.
  • Deploy a Microservice: Build a small REST API in Spring Boot or Django and containerize it using Docker. Try creating a basic CI/CD pipeline using Jenkins.
  • Learn Observability: Spend an hour understanding what Grafana or Prometheus does. "Monitoring" is a critical part of maintaining resilient systems at Akamai.
  • SkillBloom Resource: Download our "Networking & System Design Interview Kit" from the PDF section for common Akamai technical questions.

FlexBase & Company Benefits

Akamai FlexBase: Akamai trusts its employees to work in ways that suit them best. You have the flexibility to work from home, in an office, or a combination of both. Akamai also provides comprehensive health, well-being, and financial benefits to support your life beyond work.
