Entry Level Tech Role

Customer Experience Engineer, L1

HackerRank - Matching Developers based on Skill, not Pedigree

Location: Hybrid in Bangalore, India | Department: Go To Market

Drive Overview

HackerRank helps companies like NVIDIA, Amazon, and Microsoft hire and upskill the next generation of developers. Software has entered an era where humans and AI build side by side, and HackerRank is at the forefront of this shift. We are looking for a Customer Experience Engineer, L1 to join our team in Bangalore. In this hybrid role, you will own the first response on inbound technical tickets, resolve complex queries, and utilize AI tools to support the world's most innovative engineering teams.

Experience Level 0 - 3 Years
Target Audience Freshers Welcome
Work Schedule 24x7 Rotational Shifts

Your Role & Responsibilities

This is the entry point into a defined engineering progression. CXEs who build strong technical depth and communication discipline have a clear path to becoming Technical Support Engineers (TSE 2).

  • First Contact Ownership: Own first response on all inbound tickets within SLA. Resolve product usage questions, configuration issues, and standard troubleshooting independently.
  • Intelligent Triage: Triage and escalate complex tickets to CXE 2 with complete context: reproduction steps, relevant logs, and a clear description of troubleshooting efforts.
  • Queue Management: Monitor the queue actively during your shift. Flag stalled tickets or anything at risk of an SLA breach before it happens. Maintain end-to-end ticket hygiene.
  • AI Utilization: Use AI tools actively to resolve tickets faster, identify similar past issues, and document solutions for the knowledge base.
  • Shift Operations: Work as part of a team providing 24x7 global customer coverage on a rotational shift schedule covering mornings, evenings, and weekends.

Market Trend: Support in the AI Era

Technical support is no longer about reading from scripts. In the era of AI and advanced SaaS platforms, Level 1 engineers are expected to read API logs, debug network tabs, and utilize AI copilots to drastically reduce resolution times. Working at HackerRank provides direct exposure to the tools and workflows used by top global engineering teams, making this an excellent launchpad for a career in Site Reliability, DevOps, or Software Development.

What We Are Looking For

REST APIs SQL Queries Browser DevTools Log Analysis Ticketing/SLA AI Tooling

Technical Skills

  • Experience: 0 to 3 years in a technical support or customer-facing technical role. Strong freshers with demonstrated technical aptitude are welcome.
  • API Knowledge: Basic understanding of REST APIs: HTTP methods, status codes, and how to read a request/response.
  • Debugging: Ability to read and interpret logs, browser developer tools, and error messages before escalating.
  • Database: Familiarity with SQL—enough to write and run a basic query or understand what a query is checking.
  • AI Fluency: Comfortable using AI tools to accelerate investigation and resolution, not just for drafting responses.

Bonus Skills (Even better if you have...)

  • Experience with Zendesk, Freshdesk, Pylon, or similar ticketing tools.
  • Used Postman or similar tools to test or inspect API calls.
  • Built any small scripts, automations, or tools for yourself.
  • Background in SaaS platforms, HR tech, or developer tools.

Expert Preparation Guide for HackerRank CXE

1. Master the Network Tab & APIs

Because HackerRank provides an online IDE and assessment platform, many customer issues will be related to browser connections or API timeouts. Practice opening Chrome DevTools, navigating to the Network tab, and inspecting HTTP requests. Know the difference between a 400 Bad Request, 401 Unauthorized, and 500 Internal Server Error.

2. Basic SQL Proficiency

You need to be able to pull data to verify customer claims. Ensure you are comfortable with basic SELECT statements, WHERE clauses, and simple JOINs. If a customer says "my test report didn't generate," you should know how to query the database for their specific session ID.

3. Ownership & De-escalation (The Soft Skills)

The JD specifically asks for someone who is "Empathetic and composed... You do not escalate tension, you de-escalate it." Prepare stories using the STAR method about a time you handled a frustrated user, took ownership of a messy problem, or improved a process.

The Right Fit

You take ownership before you’re asked to, communicate clearly even when you don’t have all the answers, and treat every ticket as an opportunity to get better. You understand that world-class support is not just about resolving tickets. It’s about making customers feel confident in HackerRank, and that starts with you.
