Amdocs System Analyst – Pune (Job ID: 200188)

Company Overview

Amdocs is a global leader in telecom and media software and services. With revenue of $5.00 billion in fiscal 2024, Amdocs empowers communications and media providers to transform and deliver next-generation experiences. Listed on the NASDAQ Global Select Market, Amdocs operates worldwide, helping customers navigate their digital transformation.

Job Summary

Location: India – Pune (Amdocs Site)

Job Type: Full-time

Travel Required: Minimal

Job ID: 200188

Managerial Role: No

Job Responsibilities

  • Gather and analyze client requirements and gaps.
  • Prepare configuration documents and specifications.
  • Configure the solution using system tools per project needs.
  • Execute and maintain detailed test plans.
  • Assist in training preparation (functional and technical).
  • Resolve configuration or implementation issues as needed.
  • Support the solution throughout the project lifecycle.

Required Skills

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related Engineering field.
  • Basic programming and scripting skills.
  • Basic knowledge of SQL.
  • Good understanding of Unix.
  • Familiarity with Amdocs Ordering or Billing software is a plus.

Bonus Points For

  • Prior experience with other commercial packages (e.g., SAP, Oracle, SuccessFactors).
  • Hands-on experience in configuration with telecom/IT software products.

Why Join Amdocs?

  • Work in a multicultural, dynamic, and fast-paced environment.
  • Be part of a world-class team working on cutting-edge telecom software.
  • Gain opportunities to grow in a leading global company.
  • Enjoy inclusive work culture and strong learning environment.
