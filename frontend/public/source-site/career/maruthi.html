Strict Eligibility Criteria

Note: Maruti Suzuki follows a highly stringent academic screening process. No rounding off of marks is allowed (e.g., 64.99% will be rejected).

  • Schooling: Minimum 65% marks or 6.5 CGPA in 10th & 12th / Diploma.
  • B.E / B.Tech (GET): Minimum 65% marks aggregate or 6.5 CGPA till the recent semester.
  • M.E / M.Tech (PGET): Minimum 60% marks aggregate or 6.0 CGPA till the recent semester.
  • Education Type: Only FULL-Time and Regular education is eligible.

Disqualification Criteria (Not Eligible):

  • Any education from Distance learning or open school.
  • Students pursuing dual degrees.
  • Students with pending back papers at the time of the selection process.
  • Medical: Students having vision-related issues and/or color blindness are strictly not eligible for this manufacturing role.

Maruti Suzuki Selection Process

The recruitment process is robust and designed to assess technical depth, cognitive ability, and cultural fit:

1. Online Test

A comprehensive assessment divided into three sections: Technical (Core Mechanical/Tool & Die concepts), Aptitude (Quant, Logical, Verbal), and Psychometric (Behavioral and safety-oriented decision making).

2. Personal Interview Round

An in-depth Face-to-Face or Virtual panel interview evaluating both Technical expertise (GD&T, Manufacturing Processes) and HR/Cultural fit.

3. Medical Verification & Offer

Successful candidates will undergo a strict pre-employment medical check-up (including color vision tests) before final offers are rolled out for tentative joining in July 2026.

Expert Prep Guide: Tool & Die Engineering

1. Core Technical Concepts

Since this role is specific to "Tool & Die," you must brush up on GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing), Sheet Metal Operations (Blanking, Piercing, Bending), Plastic Injection Molding, and Jigs & Fixtures design.

2. The Psychometric Assessment

Automobile giants prioritize safety and process adherence. During the psychometric test, answer consistently and lean towards choices that show you value teamwork, safety protocols, and long-term stability over reckless risk-taking.

3. CAD / CAM Tools

While not explicitly mentioned, demonstrating familiarity with tools like CATIA, SolidWorks, or Siemens NX during your interview will prove you are ready to hit the ground running in a design and tooling environment.
