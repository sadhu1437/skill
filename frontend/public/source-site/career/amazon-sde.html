Software Dev Engineer I (SDE I)

Amazon - Innovating and Building on Behalf of Our Customers

Location: ADCI - Karnataka (Bengaluru) | Job ID: 10454435

Drive Overview

At Amazon, we hire the best minds in technology to innovate and build on behalf of our customers. Customer obsession is part of our company DNA. Our Software Development Engineers (SDEs) use cutting-edge technology to solve complex problems and see the impact of their work first-hand across millions of customers, sellers, and products around the world. We are looking for passionate individuals from the 2025 and 2026 graduating batches who thrive in managing ambiguity and operating at a pace where development cycles are measured in weeks, not years.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Grads Only
Role Level SDE I (Entry Level)
Primary Hub Karnataka (Bengaluru)

Your Role & Responsibilities

As an SDE I, you will chart your own path at Amazon, working on solutions that run predictions on distributed systems at an incredible scale and speed.

  • System Architecture: Design and build innovative technologies in a large distributed computing environment.
  • Distributed Storage: Build distributed storage, index, and query systems that are scalable, fault-tolerant, low cost, and easy to manage/use.
  • Problem Solving: Design and code the right solutions starting with broadly defined, highly ambiguous problems.
  • Agile Execution: Work in an agile environment to deliver high-quality software alongside experienced cross-disciplinary Amazonians.
  • Innovation: Conceive, design, and bring innovative products and services to market from scratch.

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or above in computer science, computer engineering, or a related field.
  • CS Fundamentals: Deep knowledge of computer science fundamentals such as Object-Oriented Design (OOD), algorithm design, data structures, problem solving, and complexity analysis (Big O notation).

Expert Preparation Guide for Amazon SDE I

1. Master Data Structures & Algorithms

Amazon's technical bar is exceptionally high. You must be comfortable with complex problem-solving on platforms like LeetCode. Focus heavily on Trees, Graphs, Dynamic Programming, and HashMap implementations. You will be tested on your ability to optimize for both Time and Space complexity.

2. Amazon's Leadership Principles (LPs)

Amazon interviews are heavily behavioral, relying on their 16 Leadership Principles. You must frame your past projects and experiences using the STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Pay special attention to "Customer Obsession", "Learn and Be Curious", "Deliver Results", and "Dive Deep".

3. Basics of Distributed Systems

While SDE I candidates are not expected to design AWS from scratch, understanding the basics of Load Balancing, Caching, Database Sharding, and Microservices will make you stand out massively. Be prepared to discuss how you would scale a simple application.
