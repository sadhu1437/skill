Non-Tech Operational Role

Associate, ML Data Operations

Amazon GO-AI Operations - Powering Amazon Robotics

Location: Hyderabad (A85) | Job ID: 3134242

Drive Overview

The Data Auditing Operations team at Amazon provides critical human intelligence to Amazon Fulfillment facilities. The goal is to enable hands-free active stowing through visual audits on videos and images. As an Associate in ML Data Operations, you will be supporting Amazon Internal Fulfillment Technologies & Robotics teams. By thoroughly reviewing and auditing brief videos (15-20 seconds), your work will directly increase the effectiveness of automated processes and maintain high inventory accuracy across global fulfillment centers.

Role Type Contract (6 Months)
Eligibility Any Bachelor's Degree
Work Schedule 24x7 Rotational Shifts

Your Role & Responsibilities

This is a fast-paced operational role that relies on extreme attention to detail and prolonged focus on screen-based visual data.

  • Video Auditing: Watch several hundred brief videos (15-20 seconds) per shift of stowing actions at fulfillment centers.
  • Data Verification: Verify or mark the location of products using specific tools, maintaining the highest level of accuracy and speed.
  • Quality Control: Make the best use of human judgment combined with tools to indicate the activity captured, aiding in defect reduction.
  • Target Achievement: Meet incremental targets and goals focused on quality (accuracy) and productivity (speed).
  • Sustained Focus: Ensure 6.8 to 7 hours of active screening/answering time per 9-hour shift, utilizing pre-defined break slots.

Market Trend: Machine Learning & Data Annotation

Artificial Intelligence and Robotics are only as smart as the data they are trained on. Roles in ML Data Operations and Annotation (RLHF/Computer Vision labeling) are becoming the foundational pillars of AI development. By joining Amazon’s GO-AI operations, you are literally teaching the robots how to "see" and "understand" complex environments. This exposure to high-volume operational efficiency is a fantastic stepping stone into the broader Tech-Ops and AI logistics industries.

Eligibility & Workplace Requirements

Shift & Work Environment: Associates work in a 24x7 environment with rotational 9-hour shifts (including night shifts, weekends, and holidays). Night shift allowances apply. The schedule includes two consecutive weekly offs (not necessarily Saturday/Sunday).

Any Graduate Attention to Detail Rotational Shifts Visual Auditing Non-Tech Role
  • Education: A completed Bachelor's degree is required.
  • Commitment: Willingness to work in a Non-tech role for a contract duration of exactly 6 months.
  • Focus: Ability to identify details from blurry or less sharp videos, requiring a high level of attention.
  • Setup (If WFH applicable): Must maintain a dedicated, well-lit workspace. Workspace and data must remain highly confidential. Must be willing to switch ON laptop camera during virtual meetings.
  • Flexibility: Readiness to come to the office for a few days when required (even if temporarily working from home).

Expert Preparation Guide for GO-AI Operations

1. Emphasize "Attention to Detail"

During assessments or interviews, Amazon wants to know if you can maintain focus during repetitive tasks. Be prepared to share examples of how you stay organized, double-check your work, or notice small discrepancies that others miss.

2. Flexibility & Work Ethic

The job description clearly states a 24x7 environment with rotational shifts and a 6-month contract. Ensure you communicate your absolute comfort with night shifts, weekend work, and metric-driven (productivity + accuracy) environments.

3. Understanding the "Why"

Know why this role exists: To train Amazon's Robotics and automated stowing systems. If asked, explain that human judgment is currently required to resolve ambiguities (like blurry footage or misplaced items) so the AI can learn the correct operational patterns.
