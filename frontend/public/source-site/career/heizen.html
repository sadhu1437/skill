What You Will Work On

At Heizen, we value execution and reliability. You won't just be writing code; you will be improving product quality through deep architectural understanding.

  • Product Lifecycle: Understand how products work from the ground up and find issues before users do.
  • Automation: Design thoughtful automation frameworks to ensure fast-moving releases stay stable.
  • Real-World Systems: Build systems that perform in complex, non-ideal real-world conditions.
  • Collaboration: Work directly with founders Aman Arora, Abhilasha Singh, and Nijansh Verma.

Heizen Selection Process Roadmap

The recruitment drive is rigorous and will be held onsite at PDEU (subject to confirmation) on 25th–26th April 2026.

R0: Application Screening

Review of academic performance, competitive programming profiles, and project depth.

R1: AI-Based Interview

Initial automated screening focusing on logic, communication, and technical basics.

R2 & R3: Offline Technical Interviews

Deep-dive sessions focusing on Data Structures, System Design (for 2025 batch), and problem-solving approaches.

R4: Cultural Fit Assessment

Final conversation to ensure alignment with Heizen's fast-shipping and quality-first mindset.

Expert Preparation Guide for Heizen

1. Competitive Programming (CP)

Since the founders are competitive programmers, expect logic puzzles and optimization questions. Master Bit Manipulation, Dynamic Programming, and Graph Algorithms.

2. Quality & Reliability

Heizen focuses on "finding issues before users do." Be ready to explain how you test your code. Understand Unit Testing, Integration Testing, and Edge Case Analysis.

3. System Design (2025 Batch)

Review how to build scalable backend systems. Practice designing "Fast-moving software products" that maintain 99.9% reliability.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Founders' Vision: Research the background of the founders (Aman, Abhilasha, Nijansh) to understand the "Shipping Fast" culture.
  • GitHub Polishing: Ensure your projects aren't just "Hello World." Show real-world utility and well-documented testing processes.
  • Algorithm Practice: Focus on LeetCode Medium/Hard or Codeforces Div 2 problems.
