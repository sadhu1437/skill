Drive Overview

Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu India LLP is hiring driven problem-solvers to join their Engineering, AI & Data practice in Bengaluru. As an Associate Analyst (Cloud Infrastructure), you will empower and drive mission-critical solutions, modernizing existing systems and supporting SLA-driven service delivery. Your Role & Responsibilities

You will act as the operational backbone for mission-critical cloud infrastructure, ensuring stability and uninterrupted service delivery in a high-stakes 24x7 environment.

  • Monitoring & Triage: Monitor ServiceNow dashboards and other operational monitoring tools continuously.
  • Incident Management: Track, log, and manage incidents, alerts, and service requests meticulously.
  • SOP Adherence: Follow defined Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and escalation matrices to ensure timely resolution.
  • SLA Compliance: Ensure strict adherence to agreed SLAs and operational processes.
  • Collaboration: Coordinate effectively with internal teams for incident resolution and follow-up activities.
  • Reporting: Maintain accurate records of incidents, actions taken, and prepare operational reports as required.
  • Shift Operations: Support shift handovers and maintain continuity of operations. Escalate critical issues promptly.

Market Insight: Cloud Ops & ITSM

As major corporations shift their entire ecosystems to the Cloud, maintaining uptime is more critical than ever. IT Service Management (ITSM) forms the foundation of this stability. Platforms like ServiceNow are the industry standard for incident and alert management. Joining Deloitte in this capacity provides you with high-value, transferable skills in Enterprise IT Operations, setting up a long-term career path in Cloud Support, Site Reliability, and Service Management.

Expert Preparation Guide for Deloitte Cloud Analyst

1. ITSM & ITIL Fundamentals

The job description repeatedly mentions incidents, alerts, service requests, and SLAs. Familiarize yourself with basic ITIL (IT Infrastructure Library) terminology. Know the clear difference between an "Incident" (an unplanned interruption) and a "Service Request" (a user requesting something new).

2. ServiceNow Awareness

ServiceNow is the specific operational dashboard mentioned. While you don't need to be an administrator, watching a 10-minute YouTube video on "How ServiceNow Incident Management works" will give you a massive advantage during HR or technical screening rounds.

3. Behavioral Readiness (Rotational Shifts)

Big 4 firms need assurance of your reliability. Be prepared to confidently answer questions about your willingness to work night shifts and handle the pressure of critical, time-sensitive escalations. Use the STAR method to describe a time when you managed a high-stress situation or met a tight deadline.
