AI/ML Engineer (Freshers / 2+ Yrs)

L&T Energy Offshore - Accelerating Digital Transformation in EPC

Location: AMTP | Role Type: Full-time

Drive Overview

L&T Energy Offshore is accelerating its digital transformation by deploying advanced AI/ML solutions across Engineering, Procurement, Construction (EPC), Safety, and Compliance. We are looking for an enthusiastic AI/ML Engineer who can build, deploy, and optimize intelligent systems such as chatbots, video analytics pipelines, and engineering document automation tools. This role is ideal for freshers with strong AI fundamentals or engineers with up to 2+ years of experience in AI/ML, data science, or Azure AI services.

Experience Level 0 - 2+ Years (Freshers)
Primary Stack Python, Azure AI, YOLO
Target Degree B.E/B.Tech/M.Tech (CS/AI/ML)

Key Responsibilities & Impact

You will build autonomous systems and AI agents that solve real-world, safety-critical challenges in large-scale EPC projects.

1. Azure AI & Chatbot Development

  • Build AI-powered chatbots using Azure OpenAI, Azure Bot Service, Prompt Flow, and Cognitive Search.
  • Fine-tune LLMs (GPT-4/4o, Phi-3, etc.) using domain-specific datasets for engineering tasks.
  • Integrate chatbots with Teams, SharePoint, and Azure Logic Apps.

2. Video Analytics for Safety & Operations

  • Develop and deploy deep learning-based video analytics models for PPE detection, safety violations, and hazard identification.
  • Implement vision models (YOLOv8/Detectron2) for object detection and anomaly tracking.
  • Integrate video pipelines into the Azure ecosystem (Blob → Functions → AI Models → Dashboards).

3. P&ID Processing & Document Automation

  • Build systems for automatic extraction of MTO (Material Take-Off) from P&IDs using Computer Vision and OCR (Azure Document Intelligence).
  • Contribute to AI agent workflows for cross-verifying P&IDs, 3D models, and line lists using RAG and NLP.
  • Fine-tune models for Engineering Drawing Audit Automation.

Market Trend: AI in Heavy Engineering & EPC

The Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) industry is undergoing a paradigm shift. Companies are leveraging Agentic AI, Computer Vision, and Digital Twins to ensure workplace safety and automate the processing of complex engineering blueprints (P&IDs). Joining L&T Energy Offshore places you at the intersection of core industrial engineering and cutting-edge GenAI. Mastering tools like YOLOv8 for hazard detection and Azure OpenAI for engineering compliance is highly lucrative and sets a robust foundation for a career in Industrial AI.

Strict Eligibility & Technical Requirements

Mandatory Education: Must come from a Computer Science or AI/ML specialization. B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech in CS/AI/ML is mandatory.
Python (Mandatory) PyTorch / TensorFlow Azure OpenAI / AI Foundry YOLOv8 / Detectron2 RAG / LLMs DevOps & APIs
  • AI/ML Fundamentals: Strong understanding of ML algorithms, deep learning, transformers, CNNs, and RNNs.
  • Azure Ecosystem: Experience with Azure Cognitive Services (Vision, Document Intelligence, Search), Azure ML, and Azure Functions.
  • Computer Vision: Hands-on with YOLOv8, OpenCV, and OCR engines (Azure OCR / Form Recognizer).
  • NLP: LLM fine-tuning, RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation), and prompt engineering.
  • Software Engineering: Good understanding of DevOps, APIs, integration workflows, and basic SQL/NoSQL.

Expert Preparation Guide for L&T AI/ML Role

1. Master the RAG Pipeline & Azure AI

L&T relies heavily on the Azure ecosystem. Be prepared to explain how you would build a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using Azure Cognitive Search to query complex engineering documents (like PDFs or P&IDs). Understand chunking, embeddings, and vector databases.

2. Computer Vision & YOLOv8

The JD explicitly mentions PPE detection and safety hazard identification. Brush up on how object detection models like YOLOv8 work. Be ready to discuss how you would prepare a dataset, fine-tune the model, and deploy it for real-time video analytics.

3. Agentic Workflows & OCR

Research how OCR (Optical Character Recognition) extracts metadata from engineering drawings. If asked in an interview, explain how you would combine Azure Document Intelligence with an LLM agent to cross-verify compliance clauses automatically.
