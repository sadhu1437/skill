Associate Software Engineer

Accuris - Accelerating Innovation in Engineering Workflows

Location: Bengaluru | Hybrid Mode (2 Days in Office)

Drive Overview

Accuris, formerly known as S&P Global’s Engineering Solutions division, launched as a standalone powerhouse in May 2023. For over 60 years, we have been the backbone of the engineering ecosystem, valued globally for standards content and workflow solutions like Engineering Workbench, Goldfire, and Haystack. We are looking for bright, fresh talent to join our Bengaluru hub as an Associate Software Engineer. If you are a go-getter ready to commit to building enterprise-grade applications, Accuris offers the perfect launchpad.

Eligible Batches 2022 to 2026
Primary Stack Python / Java / Angular
Experience Fresher (0 Years)

Strict Eligibility & Commitment Criteria

Please note that Accuris has highly specific requirements for this role. Do not apply if you do not meet the following:

  • Academics: A minimum of 60% marks in B.E/B.Tech, Class XII, and Class X.
  • Batch: Candidates must have graduated not earlier than 2022 (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 batches are eligible).
  • Degree: Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related fields like Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning.
  • Service Commitment: Must be willing to commit to 2 years with the organization.

Your Role & Responsibilities

This is a foundational role. You will spend your initial months learning Accuris's proprietary products and tech stack before independently shipping code.

  • Mentored Learning: Learn the technologies and programming languages used in the suite of products and applications under the guidance of senior team members.
  • Software Development: Design, code, and develop solutions in a time-bound manner for assigned tasks.
  • Agile Collaboration: Work closely with the development team to understand internal processes and industry best practices.
  • Innovation: Bring a "go-getter" attitude to explore solutions beyond standard documentation.

Required Skills & Tech Stack

Python Java Angular AI / ML (Bonus) Big Data (Bonus) Cloud Tech (Bonus)
  • Programming: Reasonable programming skills in either Python, Java, or Angular.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent problem-solving, analytical skills, and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Team Player: Good at communication, collaboration, and taking constructive feedback.
  • Added Advantage: Any academic exposure or projects involving Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data, or Cloud technologies will highly boost your profile.

Expert Preparation Guide for Accuris

1. Pick Your Strongest Lane

Since the JD asks for "Python, Java OR Angular," don't try to be a master of all three during the interview. If you pick Java, ensure you know OOPS and Collections perfectly. If Angular, understand components and state management.

2. Leverage the "Added Advantage"

Accuris builds engineering workflow tools. Mentioning how Cloud deployments (AWS/Azure) or Big Data analysis can speed up engineering searches will show you understand their product ecosystem.

3. The 2-Year Commitment Mindset

During HR rounds, emphasize your desire for "stability and deep product learning." Accuris wants candidates who will stay and master their complex legacy and modern systems over the next two years.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Product Research: Google "Accuris Engineering Workbench" to understand the kind of B2B software you will be working on.
  • AI/ML Project: If you have an AI/ML project in your portfolio, put it at the very top of your resume. This is a massive differentiator for this specific role.
