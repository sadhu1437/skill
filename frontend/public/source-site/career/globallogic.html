Non-Coding AI Role

Associate Analyst

GlobalLogic (A Hitachi Group Company) - Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Location: Gurgaon, India | Req ID: IRC299794 | On-site

Drive Overview

GlobalLogic, a Hitachi Group Company, is a leader in digital engineering. We help brands across the globe design and build innovative products, platforms, and digital experiences. We are looking for motivated, intelligent, and diligent individuals to join our Gurgaon team as an Associate Analyst. This role focuses on the labeling and annotation of data for the development of AI and machine learning models. No prior knowledge of data annotation is required as thorough training will be provided, making this the ideal gateway into the booming AI/ML industry.

Experience Level 0 - 1 Years (Fresher)
Target Degree Any Bachelor's Degree
Work Schedule 24/7 Rotational Shifts

Your Role & Responsibilities

As an Associate Analyst, you are the human intelligence teaching AI how to understand the world. Your attention to detail directly impacts model accuracy.

  • Data Annotation: Manually label data points including text, audio, video, and images as per strict guidelines and instructions.
  • Quality Control: Maintain the consistency and correctness of labeled data by adhering to established standards and guidelines.
  • Content Creation: Describe pictures, videos, or audio clips using appropriate grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, and sentence structure.
  • Data Gathering: Assist in gathering non-personal data for AI model development (e.g., Audio clips, Images of vehicles, receipts, buildings).
  • Communication: Comprehend directives and accurately receive/interpret audio messages to execute tasks efficiently.
  • Troubleshooting: Use strong problem-solving skills to navigate complex and large-scale datasets or annotation challenges.

Market Trend: Data Annotation & AI Training

Behind every smart AI model (like ChatGPT, Gemini, or self-driving cars) are thousands of hours of high-quality human-labeled data. The process of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) and Data Annotation is the fastest-growing non-coding tech segment globally. Joining GlobalLogic in this role puts you at the foundation of Generative AI and Computer Vision development. It is a fantastic entry point for freshers from non-engineering backgrounds to build a career in the Tech and AI sector.

Eligibility & Skill Requirements

Work Environment Notice: Candidates must be absolutely open to working in a 24/7 Environment (involving rotational day and night shifts) directly from the Gurgaon office.
Any Graduate Data Labeling Detail-Oriented English Proficiency MS Office / G-Suite
  • Education: Bachelor's degree in ANY discipline.
  • Experience: 0 to 1 years (Freshers highly encouraged to apply).
  • Computer Skills: Proficiency in operating laptops/desktops, basic knowledge of computer operations, and familiarity with MS Office / Google Suite.

Expert Preparation Guide for Associate Analyst

1. Attention to Detail & Grammar

Since you will be writing descriptions for images and videos, your English grammar, punctuation, and sentence structure must be flawless. The initial assessment will likely include a rigorous reading comprehension and error-spotting test.

2. Understanding AI & Data Labeling

While no prior experience is needed, knowing the basics will impress the interviewers. Read up briefly on what Data Annotation, Bounding Boxes (for images), and Sentiment Analysis (for text) are. Show a strong interest in how data powers Machine Learning.

3. Adaptability to Repetitive Work

Data labeling can be highly repetitive. During HR rounds, emphasize your patience, focus, and ability to maintain high quality standards over long hours. Highlight your readiness to work in 24/7 rotational shifts.
