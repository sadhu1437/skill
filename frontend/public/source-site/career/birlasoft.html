Birlasoft - Empowering Communities Through Design-Thinking

Role: Consultant (Oracle Transportation Management) | Location: PAN India / Pune

Drive Overview

Birlasoft, a global leader at the forefront of Cloud, AI, and Digital technologies, seamlessly blends domain expertise with enterprise solutions. As part of the multibillion-dollar CKA Birla Group, Birlasoft is inviting enthusiastic engineering and management graduates from the 2025 and 2026 batches to join their Oracle Transportation Management (OTM) consulting team. In this techno-functional role, you will implement and configure enterprise supply chain solutions while actively leveraging cutting-edge Generative AI tools to solve real-world logistics challenges.

Target Batches 2025 & 2026 Grads
Target Degree B.Tech / MBA
Core Domain Oracle Cloud & AI

Your Role & Responsibilities

This is a Techno-Functional role. You will act as the bridge between technical software architecture and core business supply chain processes.

  • Enterprise Consulting: Relate product functionality to business processes and advise customers on meeting their various business scenarios.
  • Implementation: Learn and apply Oracle Transportation Management (OTM) functionality, assisting in requirement gathering, solution design, and testing.
  • Support & Integration: Support production incident resolution, participate in system integration, and conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
  • AI Utilization: Leverage AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) to improve productivity, documentation, and AI-assisted coding/debugging.
  • Documentation: Prepare highly detailed functional specifications, technical documentation, and user guides for global clients.

Market Trend: GenAI & Supply Chain Cloud

Global supply chains are shifting toward AI-driven logistics and cloud modernization. Oracle Transportation Management (OTM) is an industry-leading ERP module used by Fortune 500 companies to optimize complex global shipping. By mastering OTM alongside Generative AI & Prompt Engineering, you position yourself in a highly specialized, recession-proof consulting niche that commands top-tier salaries in the IT consulting sector.

Eligibility & Skill Requirements

SQL & Databases Supply Chain Basics Generative AI Prompt Engineering APIs / JSON / XML

Academics & Experience

  • Engineering Grads: B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, Mechanical, or Industrial Engineering.
  • Management Grads: MBA in Operations, Supply Chain, Systems, or Business Analytics.
  • Batch: 2025/2026 graduates or recent graduates with excellent academic performance.

Preferred & AI Skills

  • Technical Foundation: Basic knowledge of SQL, databases, APIs, XML, and JSON integration concepts.
  • Domain Understanding: Exposure to Supply Chain, Logistics, or ERP business processes is an advantage.
  • Generative AI Expertise: Demonstrated curiosity and practical exposure to modern AI tools (ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini). Ability to execute AI-assisted coding, debugging, and prompt engineering.
  • Soft Skills: Strong analytical, logical reasoning, and excellent verbal/written communication skills.

Expert Preparation Guide for Birlasoft

1. Understand the "Techno-Functional" Bridge

You must balance tech and business. For CS/IT students, read up on basic Supply Chain concepts (Logistics, Freight, Inventory). For MBA students, ensure you understand what APIs are and how relational databases (SQL) store corporate data.

2. Showcase GenAI Competency

The JD specifically highlights "Prompt engineering fundamentals" and "AI-assisted coding." During the interview, explain how you use LLMs to speed up your learning, generate boilerplate code, or debug errors while keeping data privacy and AI ethics in mind.

3. Oracle OTM Awareness

Spend 20 minutes researching Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and the basic purpose of Oracle Transportation Management (OTM). Mentioning this specific platform will show genuine interest in the role.
