Fresher - Graduate Engineer

Zyphra Tech Solutions - Matching Talent with Global Innovation

Location: Hyderabad, India | Type: Full-Time

Drive Overview

Zyphra Tech Solutions is a fast-growing staffing and technology services firm providing end-to-end recruitment and workforce solutions across multiple industries. We are currently seeking enthusiastic and motivated Graduate Engineer Trainees (GETs) to join our Hyderabad team. This role is a perfect launchpad for freshers eager to apply their technical education to real-time projects within a fast-paced technology ecosystem.

Target Batch 2024, 2025 & 2026
Role Level Fresher (0-1 Yrs)
Location Hyderabad (Telangana)

What You Will Do

GETs at Zyphra Tech work at the intersection of technical analysis and project execution.

  • Project Support: Assist senior engineers in designing, developing, and testing various project modules.
  • Technical Troubleshooting: Analyze technical issues and provide viable solutions under mentorship.
  • Continuous Learning: Actively participate in the company's intensive training programs and technical workshops.
  • Documentation: Follow company standards to maintain records, prepare reports, and document project lifecycle activities.
  • Execution: Support the execution of real-time projects while adapting to new tools and industry frameworks.

Eligibility & Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (B.Tech / BE) from a recognized university.
  • Experience: 0–1 year of experience (Freshers are highly encouraged).
  • Foundations: Basic understanding of engineering concepts and analytical thinking.
  • Communication: Good written and verbal communication skills for team collaboration.
  • Preferred: Prior internship experience or projects involving programming/technical tools is an advantage.

Zyphra Tech Selection Process

1. Application & Resume Screening

Evaluation of academic scores and technical projects listed in the resume.

2. Aptitude & Technical Assessment

A round focusing on logical reasoning, basic engineering concepts, and problem-solving scenarios.

3. Technical Interview

In-depth discussion on final year projects, engineering fundamentals, and your preferred technical stack.

4. HR & Managerial Round

Final round assessing behavioral fit, career goals, and communication depth.

Expert Preparation Guide for GET Role

1. Core Engineering Fundamentals

Master the basics of your core branch (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE). Be ready to explain "How it works" rather than just providing definitions.

2. Project Deep-Dive

Be prepared to discuss your internship or academic projects using the STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Highlight the tools used.

3. Communication Polish

Since Zyphra Tech deals with staffing and services, your ability to explain complex concepts clearly is a key KPI. Practice your "Tell me about yourself" pitch.
