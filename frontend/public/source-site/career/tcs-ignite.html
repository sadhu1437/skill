TCS B.Sc Ignite Hiring - 2026 Batch

Tata Consultancy Services - Building the World’s Largest AI-Led Tech Services Company

In-Centre Test Date: 26 August 2026 | Location: PAN India

Drive Overview

TCS B.Sc Ignite Hiring is an exclusive opportunity for candidates with a science or vocational background from the Batch of 2026. As part of its AI-powered transformation, TCS is seeking and nurturing science graduates who demonstrate strong potential, aptitude, and passion for technology. Through this program, you will transition from "Science to Software," building successful and rewarding careers in the IT industry alongside traditional engineering professionals.

Target Batch 2026 Graduates Only
Eligible Degrees BCA / B.Sc / B.Voc
Test Format In-Centre (TCS iON)

Why Join TCS Ignite?

Students who excel in the selection process gain access to TCS’ distinctive ‘Science to Software’ immersive training program.

  • Accelerated Compensation: A structure designed to reward performance, offering potential salary increases of up to 2.5x within 1–2 years.
  • Higher Education Support: Pursue curated postgraduate programs from top Indian universities. TCS contributes 50% toward academic credit costs.
  • World-Class Training: The Initial Learning Program (ILP) bridges academic knowledge with industry-relevant IT skills.
  • Cutting-Edge Tech: Engage in developing solutions using Cloud Computing, Generative AI, Machine Learning, AR/VR, Blockchain, and Robotics.

Strict Eligibility Criteria

Your eligibility will be checked at various stages. Discrepancies will lead to disqualification.

  • Courses: 3-years full-time BCA, B.Sc (IT, CS, Maths, Data Science, Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Electronics, Cyber Security, Biochemistry), B.Voc (CS/IT).
  • Academics: Minimum aggregate of 50% or above (or equivalent CGPA) in Class X, XII, Diploma, and Graduation.
  • Backlogs: Strictly NO active backlogs are permitted at the time of the selection process or joining.
  • Gap in Education: The overall academic gap should not exceed 24 months until the highest qualification. Must be declared in the application.
  • Age & Experience: Age 18–28 years. This is an entry-level role, but prior work experience must be declared.

Application & Test Process

The allotment of seats in the respective centres is on a ‘First-Come-First-Serve’ basis. Register early to secure your preferred city!

Step 1: NextStep Registration

Visit the TCS NextStep Portal. Register under the "IT" category, generate a Reference ID, and click "Apply for Drive". Your status must show "Application Received".

Step 2: In-Centre Assessment (120 Mins)

The test is conducted in-person by TCS iON and comprises four sections:

Numerical Ability Verbal Ability Reasoning Ability Coding (Optional)

Expert Preparation Guide for TCS Ignite

1. Mastering the NQT Foundation

The Numerical, Verbal, and Reasoning sections form the core of the TCS iON test. Focus intensely on speed and accuracy. Revise standard topics like Time & Work, Profit & Loss, Syllogisms, and Reading Comprehension.

2. The "Optional" Coding Section

While coding is marked as optional for science graduates, attempting and passing it drastically increases your chances of securing the higher-tier compensation packages immediately after your Initial Learning Program (ILP).

3. TCS iON Test Environment

Familiarize yourself with the interface. Remember that sections are often time-bound, and you usually cannot toggle between sections once submitted. Bring your physical documents and ID proof to the center as instructed.

Important Alert

Fraud Alert: TCS does NOT send job offers from unofficial email IDs (Gmail, Yahoo) and NEVER asks candidates to deposit any money for job offers. TCS is not associated with any external agency to conduct interviews.
