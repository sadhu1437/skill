Exclusive 2025 Batch Hiring

Systems Engineer - EV

EdgeVerve Systems (An Infosys Company) - Finacle Digital Banking

Locations: Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune | Hybrid Mode

Drive Overview

EdgeVerve Systems Ltd., a wholly-owned product subsidiary of Infosys (a global technology leader with over USD 15 billion in annual revenues), empowers enterprises across the globe through AI, Automation, and Banking platforms. We are currently seeking bright 2025 BE/BTech graduates to join our Finacle unit. Finacle is the industry’s top-rated digital banking solution, powering the operations of banks in over 100 countries. As a Systems Engineer, you will be at the heart of developing, testing, and deploying cutting-edge financial technology.

Target Batch 2025 Grads Only
Eligibility BE / BTech
Work Model Hybrid (3 Days Office)

Your Role & Responsibilities

You will be part of high-impact projects related to the Delivery, Testing, Development, and Support of the Finacle Product suite.

  • Customization: Modify and configure the Finacle core banking system to meet the unique regulatory and operational needs of global banking clients.
  • Implementation: Work directly with clients and integration partners to deploy banking modules smoothly into production environments.
  • Full-Cycle Engineering: Participate across all stages—from writing logic to rigorous testing and post-deployment support.
  • Agile Collaboration: Operate in a fast-paced, hybrid work environment (mandatory 3 days work-from-office).

Market Insight: The Finacle Ecosystem

Finacle is the core banking engine behind some of the world's largest banks, managing accounts for over 1 billion customers. By joining the Finacle team at EdgeVerve, you are entering the highly lucrative FinTech and Core Banking domain. Experience in customizing and implementing Tier-1 banking software is a niche skill that commands tremendous respect and high compensation across global financial hubs.

Strict Eligibility Criteria

Mandatory Document Rule: Candidates MUST possess their Consolidated Marksheet or Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC) at the time of application. Without this, your application will be immediately rejected.

  • Education: BE/BTech graduates from the 2025 batch ONLY.
  • Backlogs: Candidates who currently have any active backlogs or arrears need not apply.
  • Previous Batches: Candidates who graduated in 2024 or earlier are strictly not eligible for this specific drive.
  • Salary: Remuneration will be strictly as per Infosys/EdgeVerve company standards for Systems Engineers.

EdgeVerve Selection Process

Infosys subsidiaries follow a standardized and highly technical recruitment roadmap:

1. Online Assessment (Coding Challenge)

A rigorous test evaluating your proficiency in Data Structures, Algorithms, and logical programming (usually in Java, C++, or Python).

2. Technical Interview

A deep dive into your coding logic, understanding of databases (SQL is critical for banking software), and core engineering concepts.

3. HR Interview

Final evaluation of your communication skills, adaptability for hybrid work, and verification of your PDC/Marksheets.

Expert Preparation Guide for Finacle Roles

1. Database Mastery

Core banking systems run entirely on complex databases. Master SQL Joins, Normalization, Stored Procedures, and Transactions (ACID properties). You will likely be tested on this during the technical round.

2. Code Optimization

Banking software requires ultra-low latency. Practice solving LeetCode algorithms with a strict focus on Time and Space Complexity (Big O notation).

3. Learn Banking Tech Terminology

Familiarize yourself with basic terms like Core Banking, Retail vs. Corporate Banking, and API Gateways. Mentioning these shows you are "domain-ready."
