Your Role & Responsibilities

As a Technical Graduate Trainee, you will be the backbone of our network infrastructure, working directly with high-end Cisco devices and enterprise security protocols.

  • Infrastructure Management: Monitor, troubleshoot, and maintain large-scale network infrastructure.
  • Device Configuration: Configure and manage enterprise-grade routers, switches, and other network devices.

  • Security & Compliance: Support network security implementations and ensure compliance requirements are strictly met.
  • Diagnostics: Diagnose and resolve complex connectivity and performance issues efficiently.
  • Documentation: Maintain accurate network documentation, topologies, and performance reports.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with internal teams and global stakeholders to ensure seamless network operations.

Expert Preparation Guide for NTT

1. Deep Dive into Cisco Routing & Switching

Since CCNA and CCNP are mandatory, the technical interview will be intense. Be prepared to explain complex routing protocols (OSPF, EIGRP, BGP), STP (Spanning Tree Protocol), and how to troubleshoot routing loops or broadcast storms on enterprise switches.

2. Network Security Fundamentals

Understand how VPNs are established (IPSec, SSL) and the fundamentals of ACLs (Access Control Lists) on Cisco firewalls/routers. Interviewers will likely test your knowledge on mitigating basic network attacks (e.g., ARP spoofing, DHCP starvation).

3. Troubleshooting Scenarios

Practice the layered troubleshooting approach (OSI Model). If a user cannot connect to the internet, be ready to explain your steps logically: checking Physical layer (cables), Data Link (MAC/VLAN), Network (IP/Gateway/Routing), up to Application (DNS).
