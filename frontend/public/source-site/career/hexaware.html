Junior Management Trainee (JMT)

Hexaware Technologies - Automating the Future, Empowering Minds

Exclusive Hiring Drive for MBA Batch of 2026

Drive Overview

Hexaware Technologies Limited is a global leader in IT, BPS, and Consulting services, driven by a philosophy of "Automate Everything." We are inviting ambitious MBA graduates from the 2026 Batch to join our leadership pipeline as Junior Management Trainees (JMT). This role is designed for individuals who possess a blend of analytical rigor, leadership quotient, and the agility to thrive in a fast-paced global corporate environment. Successful candidates will be placed in Siruseri, Chennai, or other Hexaware hubs across India.

Fixed CTC ₹5.00 LPA
Target Batch 2026 Passouts
Work Location PAN India / Chennai

Your Role & Responsibilities

A JMT at Hexaware acts as a catalyst for efficiency, bridging the gap between raw data and executive decision-making.

  • Data Analysis & Dashboards: Compile data from multiple global sources to create performance dashboards for executive review.
  • Process Improvement: Apply "Lean" and "Agile" frameworks to identify bottlenecks and propose scalable solutions.
  • Stakeholder Liaison: Act as a critical communication bridge between junior staff and senior management.
  • Presentation Excellence: Build and deliver compelling, data-backed decks to senior stakeholders.
  • Agility & Pivoting: Demonstrate comfort with ambiguity and the ability to switch between functional tasks seamlessly.
  • Collaborative Projects: Work with cross-functional teams (HR, Tech, Finance) on strategic organizational goals.

Market Trend: Digital-First Management

The role of management is evolving. In 2026, managers are expected to be "Tech-Fluent." Hexaware's JMT program places you at the center of Agile Business Operations. Understanding how to use BI tools (Tableau/Power BI) and Agile methodologies is no longer optional—it is the foundation of modern leadership. This role offers the perfect launchpad for an MBA graduate to enter the high-stakes world of global IT consulting.

Detailed Selection Process

1. Initial Profile Review

Shortlisting based on your 10th/12th/Bachelor's (60%+) and your enrollment in a full-time 2-year MBA program (2026 Batch).

2. Aptitude & Logical Assessment

An online test evaluating numerical reasoning, logical thinking, and basic business awareness.

3. Technical/Managerial Interview

Discussion on your internship experiences, leadership in college fests, and proficiency in Excel/PowerPoint. Be ready for a "Business Case Study."

4. Leadership Fitment Round

Final round focusing on your Agility, Leadership Quotient, and willingness to work at any Hexaware location assigned.

Eligibility & Technical Skills

MBA (Business Management) Min 60% Academics Advanced Excel PowerPoint / Presentation Lean / Agile Basics Leadership Track
  • Education: Must be enrolled in a 2-year full-time MBA program (Batch 2026).
  • Base: Bachelor’s degree with a minimum of 60% or 6.00 CGPA.
  • Analytical: Strong proficiency in Excel (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUPs, Data Visualization).
  • Soft Skills: Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Mindset: Proactive, able to prioritize, and manage time effectively under pressure.

Expert Preparation Guide for Hexaware JMT

1. Advanced Excel Mastery

Since data reporting is a core responsibility, practice Pivot Tables, Macros, and Data Cleaning. Be prepared to explain how you would visualize a "Monthly Performance Dashboard."

2. Leadership Evidence

The JD explicitly mentions "College fests, sports, or internships." Prepare 2-3 stories using the STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) where you led a team through ambiguity.

3. Hexaware Strategy

Research Hexaware's "Cloudify Everything" and "Automate Everything" vision. Mentioning how management can drive automation will impress your interviewers.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • BI Tool Awareness: Spend 5 hours learning the basics of Power BI or Tableau. It’s mentioned as a tool for "Raw data to actionable insights."
  • Agile/Lean Course: Take a free "Agile Foundations" course to understand Scrum and Lean principles.
  • Mock Decks: Create a 5-slide PowerPoint deck on a recent business trend (e.g., AI in HR) to showcase your "Compelling Decks" ability.
  • SkillBloom Resource: Download our "Management Trainee Interview Kit" from the PDF section for specialized business case studies.
