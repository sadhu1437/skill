SW/App/Cloud Tech Support New Associate

Accenture - Technology for Operations (TFO) Team

Req ID: AIOC-S01651776 | Location: Hyderabad | Shift: Rotational

Drive Overview

Accenture is a global professional services company with leading capabilities in digital, cloud, and security. We are currently hiring for our Technology for Operations (TFO) team in Hyderabad. The TFO team acts as a trusted advisor and partner to Accenture Operations, providing innovative and secure technologies to help clients build an intelligent operating model. If you have a knack for automation, problem-solving, and managing IT systems, this entry-level role is the perfect place to kick-start your career in Intelligent Operations.

Target Audience 0 - 1 Years (Fresher)
Eligible Degrees Any Grad, BE, BCom
Primary Domain App Maintenance Automation

What You Will Do

In this role, you will be part of a team implementing software solutions, automating processes, and managing IT systems that support robust operational activities.

  • Intelligent Automation: Implement automation for reducing ticket-based and non-ticket-based effort using a combination of AI, RPA, and custom bots.
  • Incident & Ticket Management: Perform automated monitoring, predictive monitoring, self-healing, and automated service management and reporting.
  • Problem Solving: Solve routine operational problems, acting largely through precedent and referral to general guidelines under detailed instruction.
  • Data Analysis: Analyze operational data to optimize system performance and ensure workflow efficiency.
  • Operations Support: Support Major sub-deals including AHO (Application Hosting Operations) and ISMT (Infrastructure Management).
  • Collaboration: Interact effectively within your own team and direct supervisor, working as an individual contributor within a focused scope of work.

Market Trend: Intelligent IT Operations (AIOps)

The traditional IT support model is rapidly evolving. Companies are moving away from manual "break-fix" ticketing towards AIOps (Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations) and Robotic Process Automation (RPA). In this role at Accenture, you aren't just resolving tickets; you are designing and deploying the bots that automate those resolutions. This exposure to tools like Automation Anywhere sets an incredibly strong foundation for high-growth careers in DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and Automation Architecture.

Eligibility & Required Skills

Automation Anywhere RPA (Robotic Process Automation) Incident Management Analytical Skills Rotational Shifts
  • Education Requirement: Open to candidates with Any Graduation, BE, or BCom degrees.
  • Experience Level: 0 to 1 years of experience (Freshers are highly encouraged to apply).
  • Core Competency: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to troubleshoot routine applications and cloud operations.
  • Automation Skills: Familiarity or willingness to learn RPA tools, particularly Automation Anywhere.
  • Communication: Strong written and verbal communication skills to establish solid client and intra-team relationships.
  • Flexibility: Readiness to work in rotational shifts to support global operations.

Accenture Interview Preparation Guide

1. Basics of RPA & Automation Anywhere

Since the role focuses on Automation in Application Maintenance, brush up on what Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is. Understand the basics of Automation Anywhere, including what bots are, how they mimic human actions, and the difference between attended and unattended automation.

2. ITIL & Incident Management

Familiarize yourself with ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) concepts. Understand the lifecycle of an Incident: from logging a ticket, categorizing it, troubleshooting, to resolving it. Be prepared to answer scenario-based questions like, "What would you do if a critical application suddenly goes down?"

3. Behavioral & Communication Skills

Accenture places immense weight on communication. Ensure you can clearly articulate your thoughts. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to explain how you have used your analytical skills to solve a problem during a college project or previous job.
