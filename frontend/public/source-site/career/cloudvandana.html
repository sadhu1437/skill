CloudVandana - 360° Enterprise Solutions & Cutting-Edge Technologies

Location: Delhi / Indore | Level: Freshers

Drive Overview

CloudVandana assists both new and emerging startups in developing MVPs, forming teams, and gaining access to cutting-edge technologies. From application creation to modernization and maintenance, our 360° enterprise solutions cover it all. We are currently hiring energetic Freshers for the role of Associate Software Engineer across our Delhi and Indore offices. This is a fast-paced, team-oriented environment where you will build innovative solutions using Java, Web Technologies, and Salesforce.

Target Audience Freshers
Primary Stack Java, JS, SQL
Location Delhi / Indore

Your Role & Responsibilities

At CloudVandana, you will be expected to quickly learn new domains and thrive in a fast-paced environment while building scalable MVPs for startups and enterprises.

  • Software Development: Develop innovative solutions and build reusable code components.
  • Quality Assurance: Ensure code quality, performance optimization, and adhere to strict security best practices.
  • Collaboration: Collaborate with internal teams to deliver solutions and resolve complex technical issues efficiently.
  • Documentation: Document technical solutions using standard enterprise documentation practices.
  • Process Adherence: Follow and ensure that the project team adheres to the agile processes defined by the organization.
  • Adaptability: Ability to work effectively under pressure, identify ambiguity, and drive for clarity in requirements.
  • Multitasking: Capable of handling multiple tasks and assignments concurrently.

Required Skills & Technical Stack

Java HTML / CSS JavaScript / jQuery Salesforce SQL OOPS Concepts
  • Programming: Proficient in Java, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (including jQuery and related libraries).
  • Databases & Platforms: Familiarity with Salesforce ecosystem and SQL databases.
  • Core CS: Strong grasp of Object-Oriented Programming (OOPS) concepts.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Mindset: An absolute willingness to learn new technologies and frameworks.
  • Prerequisites: Must possess a personal laptop and a reliable internet connection.

Our 6-Step Recruitment Process

CloudVandana believes hiring should be transparent, respectful, and efficient for everyone involved:

Step 1: Application Review - We carefully review your resume and assess alignment with the role and technical stack.

Step 2: Initial Screening Call - A short conversation to understand your background, personal interests, and long-term career goals.

Step 3: Technical/Skill Assessment - Depending on the role, this involves a hands-on coding task, case study, or in-depth technical interview.

Step 4: Panel or Hiring Manager Interview - A deeper conversation to evaluate cultural fit, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Step 5: Offer & Documentation - Upon selection, we send the offer, sign the NDA, and complete all onboarding forms smoothly.

Step 6: Sign & Join the Team - Once you sign the offer, we officially welcome you aboard the CloudVandana team!

Expert Preparation Guide for CloudVandana

1. Java & OOPS Foundations

As Java is the core language, expect questions on the four pillars of OOPS: Polymorphism, Inheritance, Encapsulation, and Abstraction. Be prepared to write basic logic programs (e.g., String manipulations, Array sorting) during the Technical Assessment.

2. Salesforce Awareness

Even though only "familiarity" is required, knowing the basics of Salesforce CRM, Apex (which is very similar to Java), and what a cloud-based MVP is will give you a massive advantage over other candidates.

3. Front-End & SQL Basics

Brush up on how to connect a frontend (HTML/JS/jQuery) to a backend via API. Practice writing basic SQL queries involving JOIN, GROUP BY, and WHERE clauses.
