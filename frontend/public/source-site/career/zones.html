L1 - Service Desk

Zones - First Choice for IT™ | Transforming Digital Infrastructures Globally

Location: Bangalore, India | Shift: 24/7 Rotational

Drive Overview

Zones, LLC is a global Solution Provider of end-to-end IT solutions with an unmatched supply chain. Positioned to be the IT partner you need, Zones is a Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) with over 35 years of industry excellence. Operating in more than 120 countries, Zones leverages elite partnerships with Apple, Cisco, Lenovo, Microsoft, and Adobe to help enterprises scale smoothly. We are hiring L1 - Service Desk Engineers in Bangalore to deliver world-class remote technical support and resolve global hardware/software anomalies at first contact.

Annual Salary ₹3,00,000 - ₹3,50,000
Target Experience 0 - 6 Months (Freshers)

Your Role & Responsibilities

As part of our global 24/7 Service Desk squad, you are the critical first point of contact, ensuring user anomalies are diagnosed and resolved with minimal business disruption.

  • First Contact Support: Serve as the main contact for global customers seeking assistance via phone, email, and live chat.
  • Anomalies Triage: Log, track, validate, and triage technical tickets inside corporate ITSM systems while upholding defined SLAs.
  • Desktop & Device Diagnostics: Troubleshoot, configure, and install Windows operating systems, desktop peripherals, and mobile devices.
  • Remote Resolution: Execute remote diagnostic tools to guide end-users step-by-step to a fast resolution.
  • Escalation Handling: Efficiently route complex issues that cannot be resolved at first contact to L2/L3 support engineers.
  • Knowledge Management: Document troubleshooting steps and suggest modifications to the internal team knowledge base.

Market Trend: Managed Service Providers & Global Support

The global enterprise segment heavily relies on remote infrastructure optimization. With businesses operating across multiple time zones, Managed Services and IT Help Desks are utilizing automated diagnostics and AI co-pilots to increase "First Contact Resolution" (FCR). A career starting in an L1 Service Desk environment at a global Solution Provider like Zones establishes critical competencies in Windows OS internals, virtualization, basic networking protocols, and enterprise ITIL methodologies.

Skills & Qualifications Required

Windows Diagnostics ITIL Basics Active Directory MS Teams / Skype Hardware Troubleshooting Communication
  • Education: Any graduate degree completed from a recognized university.
  • Experience: 0 to 6 months of IT support or Customer service experience.
  • Technical Basics: Familiarity with Windows OS installation, configuration, basic computer networking, and desktop hardware components.
  • Collaboration Tools: Experience with enterprise communication platforms like Microsoft Teams and Skype for Business.
  • Soft Skills: Empathy, high active listening capabilities, multitasking, and exceptional verbal/written English skills.

Expert Preparation Guide for Zones

1. Master Windows Troubleshooting & Diagnostic Commands

Since Windows is the primary corporate OS, prepare for questions on fixing typical OS errors (Blue Screen of Death, corrupt registry files, profile sync issues). Know how to use the Windows Command Prompt (ipconfig, ping, tracert, nslookup, netstat) to diagnose connection faults.

2. Understand Active Directory & O365 Administration

Service desks perform hundreds of identity tasks daily. Know the basic structure of Active Directory (AD): how to reset passwords, unlock user accounts, and add a computer to a domain. Familiarity with managing Microsoft 365 licensing is a huge plus.

3. Learn the ITIL Incident Management Lifecycle

Familiarize yourself with the basic phases of an Incident. Know the difference between an Incident (an unplanned disruption to an IT service) and a Service Request (a user request for information, access, or setup). Understand why strict SLA adherence is critical.
