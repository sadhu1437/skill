Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET)

Axis Max Life Insurance - Shaping the Future of InsurTech

Location: Gurugram | Department: Digital Technology

Drive Overview

Axis Max Life Insurance Limited (formerly Max Life Insurance) is a powerhouse joint venture between Axis Bank and Max Financial Services. Consistently ranked as a "Great Place to Work," Axis Max Life is inviting fresh graduates from the 2026 Batch to join their Digital Technology hub. As a GET, you will work on mission-critical enterprise architecture, cloud-native technologies, and cutting-edge Generative AI (GenAI) experiments to redefine life insurance for millions of Indians.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Grad
Experience 0 - 1 Year
Qualification B.E/B.Tech/MCA

Your Impact & Responsibilities

This is a "learn-as-you-contribute" role where you will grow from a trainee to a core member of the Enterprise Architecture team.

  • Development: Assist in building scalable software applications, APIs, and platform components.
  • Modern Stack: Gain exposure to Cloud-native (AWS/Azure) environments and containerization (Docker).
  • Clean Code: Write maintainable, readable code with peer reviews and senior mentoring.
  • CI/CD: Contribute to technical documentation, version control, and automated deployment pipelines.
  • GenAI Experiments: Support the implementation of proof-of-concepts using emerging Generative AI technologies.
  • Collaboration: Work across development, QA, DevOps, and architecture teams to deliver high-quality solutions.

Market Trend: Technology in Insurance

The Indian life insurance sector is undergoing a massive digital overhaul. With the rise of Embedded Insurance and AI-driven underwriting, companies like Axis Max Life are no longer just finance firms—they are tech giants. Joining as a GET provides experience in a sector that achieved a gross premium of over ₹29,500 Cr last year, ensuring both high career stability and the chance to work with "Big Data" in a regulated enterprise environment.

Detailed Selection Process

1. Application & Resume Screening

Shortlisting based on academic foundation (70%+ preferred) and hands-on exposure through college projects or hackathons.

2. Online Technical Assessment

A rigorous test covering Programming Fundamentals (Java), Data Structures, Algorithms, and SQL logic.

3. Technical Interview Rounds

Focuses on OOPS concepts, SDLC awareness, and your project work. Be prepared to explain your logic on paper or a virtual whiteboard.

4. Cultural Fit & HR Round

Evaluation of your communication skills, passion for learning, and alignment with Axis Max Life's values.

Required Skills & Tech Stack

Java / OOPs Data Structures SQL / NoSQL SDLC Basics Cloud Awareness GenAI Curiosity
  • Mandatory: Strong fundamentals in Java or any Object-Oriented language.
  • Problem Solving: Basic knowledge of DSA (Arrays, Strings, HashMaps).
  • Database: Familiarity with relational database concepts and basic queries.
  • Preferred: Basic awareness of Docker, Node.js, or frontend frameworks (React/Angular).
  • Mindset: Curiosity for emerging technologies and a proactive problem-solving attitude.

Expert Preparation Guide for Axis Max Life

1. Java & OOPS Depth

Master the four pillars of OOPS: Encapsulation, Polymorphism, Abstraction, and Inheritance. Be ready to give real-world examples of how they are used in a banking/insurance context.

2. Enterprise SDLC

Understand the difference between Agile and Waterfall. Know why unit testing and code reviews are critical for large-scale enterprise platforms.

3. SQL Performance

Practice writing JOINS and understanding basic indexing. In insurance, data accuracy and retrieval speed for millions of policy records are paramount.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Generative AI Basics: Take a free course on "Prompt Engineering" or "LLM Fundamentals." Mentioning this interest will align with the job's focus on GenAI.
  • GitHub Review: Document a project that uses a Cloud Service (like an AWS S3 bucket for storage or an Azure Function).
  • Inclusion Focus: Research Axis Max Life's #ComeAsYouAre initiative. They strongly value diversity (LGBTQIA+ and PwD).
