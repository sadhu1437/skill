Top Pharmaceutical IT Role

Graduate Engineer Trainee - 2026

VIATRIS - Empowering people worldwide to live healthier at every stage of life

Location: Bengaluru | Department: IT & Information Security

Drive Overview

Viatris is a global healthcare company formed in 2020 through the combination of Mylan and Upjohn (a legacy division of Pfizer). We are committed to increasing access to high-quality medicines across the globe. Viatris is actively seeking bright Engineering Graduates from the 2026 Batch to join our Information Technology department in Bengaluru. In this role, you will be at the forefront of digital transformation in life sciences, developing bespoke applications and supporting critical cloud infrastructures using modern Agile frameworks.

Target Batch 2026 Graduates
Total CTC ₹5.00 LPA
Openings 20 Positions

Strict Eligibility Criteria

CRITICAL WARNING: Viatris follows a highly stringent academic screening policy. 59.9% will NOT be rounded up or considered. You must have exactly 60.00% or above across all educational milestones.
  • Education: B.E / B.Tech across all major tech branches (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Cyber Security, Data Science, etc.).
  • Graduation Year: Strictly 2026 pass-outs only.
  • Academics: Minimum 60% marks in 10th standard, 12th standard, and B.E/B.Tech aggregate.
  • Backlogs: No active backlogs allowed.
  • Academic Gaps: No year gaps permitted throughout your academic career.

Your Role & Responsibilities

You will operate within a high-performance Agile team, taking ownership of developing and maintaining robust web and mobile applications.

  • Full Stack Development: Develop custom Sitecore, Android, and iOS-based applications catering to business requests.
  • UI/UX Implementation: Create user interfaces that match user requirements and ensure technical feasibility.
  • Quality Engineering: Participate in testing, validating code, and assuring all user inputs are validated before submitting to back-end applications.
  • Performance Optimization: Identify bottlenecks, fix bugs, and optimize applications for scalability and latency.
  • Cloud & Infra: Apply knowledge of On-Premise and Cloud infrastructure solutions while maintaining compliance with security and federal regulatory standards.
  • Collaboration: Collaborate closely with your team, cross-functional units, and external vendors.

Preferred Skills & Tech Stack

.NET / C# Node.js / Angular Python / PowerShell Sitecore / S4 HANA Cloud Infra Generative AI
  • Programming: Basic programming/scripting knowledge in PowerShell, Python, .NET, Node.js, and Angular.
  • Design: Understanding of Bootstrap and responsive web design.
  • Systems: Basic knowledge of Windows & Linux operating systems, Networking, and Security.
  • Concepts: Solid understanding of IT Infrastructure, Web Services, Cloud-based Applications, and Agile principles.
  • Soft Skills: Demonstrated effective oral and written communication skills with strong time management.

Market Trend: IT in Life Sciences

The Pharmaceutical IT sector is experiencing exponential growth, shifting from traditional legacy systems to Generative AI, S4 HANA, and Cloud-Native platforms. Viatris uses these technologies to manage supply chains, drug discovery data, and regulatory compliance globally. Joining a Life Sciences company as an IT professional in 2026 offers immense career stability, high pay scales, and the satisfaction of knowing your code helps deliver medicines to those who need them most.

Selection Process Roadmap

The dates for the process will be communicated directly to shortlisted candidates via email.

1. Profile Shortlisting

Rigorous screening based on your Naukri job application profile. Ensure your resume highlights the 60% criteria clearly.

2. Online Test & Pre-Placement Talk

A comprehensive assessment testing aptitude, logical reasoning, and programming basics. This is usually preceded by an interactive session detailing Viatris' vision.

3. In-Person Interview (Bengaluru)

Face-to-face technical and HR rounds at the Viatris Bengaluru office evaluating your grasp on Full Stack concepts, Cloud awareness, and cultural fit.

Expert Preparation Guide for Viatris

1. Web Services & API Depth

Since the JD explicitly mentions Node.js, .NET, and Web Services, prepare to answer how you would build a secure REST API. Understand HTTP status codes, routing, and JSON data handling.

2. Scripting vs Programming

Viatris values automation. Brush up on PowerShell and Python scripting. Be ready to explain how you might automate a repetitive server task or a data deployment process.

3. Front-End Component Architecture

For the UI aspect, revise your knowledge of Angular and Bootstrap. Understand how single-page applications (SPAs) work and how to ensure forms are validated before reaching the backend.
