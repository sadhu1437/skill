Software Engineer - Microsoft

Role Overview

Company: Microsoft
Location: Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India
Date Posted: July 21, 2025
Job Number: 1848760

Overview

SharePoint helps millions of people work better together and empowers the biggest companies in the world to solve mission-critical problems. Join a team of builders and innovators committed to a low operational burden by designing for it.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical discipline
  • Proven experience coding in languages including C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Strong CS fundamentals and exceptional coding skills
  • Good communication and cross-group collaboration skills

Responsibilities

  • Lead the design/development of software and ensure its quality.
  • Define new components with an understanding of interdependencies and limitations.
  • Create prototypes and proof-of-concepts for iterative development.
  • Work effectively with product development and engineering teams.

Benefits

Industry-leading healthcare, educational resources, discounts on products and services, maternity and paternity leave, generous time away, and opportunities to network and connect.
