100% Remote / WFH Role

News Analyst

Cognizant - Shaping the Future of Algorithmic News & Media

Location: Work From Home (Hiring Office: Coimbatore) | 24x7 Shifts

Drive Overview

Cognizant is offering an exciting career opportunity for fresh graduates from the 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 batches. We are seeking passionate and detail-oriented individuals for the role of News Analyst. In this highly engaging remote position, you will be at the cutting edge of breaking news, leveraging your expertise in mass communications and digital media to help train and validate AI algorithms for our global clients.

Salary Range ₹2.75L - ₹3.25L P.A.
Target Batch 2022 to 2025 Freshers
Role Type Full Time, Permanent

Your Role & Responsibilities

As a News Analyst, you will play a crucial role in our algorithm training process. You are the human intelligence that defines what factors are important to media clients when surfacing breaking news.

  • Real-Time Monitoring: Monitor and analyze real-time data from multiple datasets continuously.
  • Content Quality Assurance: Identify, investigate, and rectify errors in automated news alerts and captions.
  • Fact-Checking: Cross-check information with social media posts and various sources for accuracy, completeness, and factual correctness.
  • Copy Editing: Write, refine, and edit captions for social media posts ensuring they are engaging and error-free.
  • Algorithmic Validation: Audit and programmatically apply business rules for data validation to improve the overall quality of the dataset output.
  • Adaptability: Stay up-to-date on new content policies, processes, and QA workflows in a rapidly changing environment.

Market Trend: Media AI & Algorithm Training

The media landscape is rapidly evolving with the integration of AI-driven news aggregators and personalized content feeds. However, algorithms require constant fine-tuning to differentiate between reliable news and misinformation. Roles like the News Analyst bridge the gap between human editorial judgment and machine learning. By auditing output and providing clean, high-quality data (RLHF - Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), you will be directly contributing to how the world consumes trustworthy news in the digital age.

Eligibility & Required Skills

Mass Communication Journalism Copy Editing Data QA Social Media Trend Analysis
  • Education: B.A in Communication/Journalism, M.A, Integrated PG, or MBA/PGDM. Only 3 or 4-year full-time degrees are accepted.
  • Target Batches: Freshers who graduated in 2022, 2023, 2024, or 2025.
  • Core Skills: Expertise in mass communications, copy editing, journalism, or similar content-heavy assignments.
  • Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong English proficiency (B2 level or higher).
  • Work Ethic: Goal-oriented mindset, strong organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple competing priorities.
  • Bonus: Professional proficiency in a foreign language is a massive plus.

Selection Process Roadmap

Cognizant follows a structured process to evaluate language proficiency and analytical skills:

1. Online Application & Assessment

Apply via the Naukri portal. Eligible candidates will receive a basic communication and language proficiency assessment.

2. Virtual Interviews

Upon clearing the assessment, you will undergo two rounds of virtual interviews focusing on your editorial judgment, news awareness, and QA mindset.

3. HR Discussion (Face-to-Face)

Candidates who clear the technical rounds will be invited for a face-to-face HR discussion (usually at the Coimbatore hiring office) to finalize the offer and discuss the 24/7 rotational shift requirements.

Expert Preparation Guide for News Analyst

1. Polish Your Grammar & Editorial Skills

Since copy editing and caption writing are core responsibilities, your written English must be flawless. Practice summarizing long news articles into punchy, 2-line social media captions without losing the factual essence.

2. Understand "Fake News" & Fact-Checking

Familiarize yourself with tools and methodologies used to fact-check information online. Interviewers will likely give you a scenario involving a viral tweet and ask how you would verify its authenticity before letting an algorithm broadcast it.

3. Readiness for a 24/7 Remote Culture

While this is a WFH role, it involves 24/7 project shifts. Be prepared to assure the HR team that you have a dedicated, distraction-free workspace and the flexibility to handle rotational timings effectively.
