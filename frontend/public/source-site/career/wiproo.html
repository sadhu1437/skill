Testing Trainee - Wipro

Driving Innovation in Health Administration & Benefits Technology

Location: Coimbatore | Shift: 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM

Drive Overview

Wipro Ltd. is a leading global provider of benefits administration, Financial Solutions, and cloud-based HR solutions. We enhance work and life through our service, technology, and data. As part of an industry-leading team, we are seeking highly motivated 2025 Graduates to join our Coimbatore facility as Testing Trainees. In this role, you will help drive results for our global clients by delivering innovative and effective software solutions within our Health Administration domain.

Target Batch 2025 Graduates
Target Degree B.E / B.Tech / MCA
Location Coimbatore (Onsite)

Your Role & Responsibilities

The Trainee is responsible for configuring and testing Wipro's Proprietary Total Benefit Administration (TBA) System to meet critical client requirements.

  • System Configuration: Utilize proprietary systems and tools to set up and configure tables and parameters in the TBA System based on analysis documents.
  • Quality Assurance: Perform thorough software testing to validate configurations and ensure defect-free deployments.
  • Database Management: Write and execute intermediate-level SQL queries (simple and complex) to retrieve and manipulate data.
  • SDLC Implementation: Apply Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) concepts to daily development and testing processes.
  • Problem Solving: Apply analytical and logical reasoning to troubleshoot system discrepancies and data anomalies.

Important Job Guidelines

Mandatory Requirement

Service Agreement: Selected candidates must sign a 12-month service agreement upon joining.

Shift Timings: Associates must be comfortable working in a specific shift schedule from 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM.

Work Location Address: Wipro, S.F.No.426/1, 426/2 and 427/1, Elcot Special Economic Zone, Vilankurichi Village, North Taluk, Coimbatore - 641004, Tamil Nadu.

In-Person Selection Process

Wipro's selection process for this drive requires candidates to attend in-person rounds at the Coimbatore facility.

1. GATE Logical Assessment

An in-person assessment conducted on a mobile platform evaluating your logical reasoning, analytical thinking, and basic technical aptitude.

2. Managerial Interview

An in-person interview focusing on your knowledge of SQL, SDLC, communication skills, and willingness to adapt to the specified shift and service agreement.

Eligibility & Technical Requirements

B.E / B.Tech / MCA SQL Queries Software Testing SDLC Knowledge Analytical Skills
  • Batch: Exclusively for Freshers graduating in 2025.
  • Education: B.E, B.Tech, or MCA degrees.
  • Database Skills: Must be able to write intermediate, simple, and complex SQL queries.
  • Testing: Good understanding of manual testing concepts and the software testing life cycle (STLC).
  • Communication: Excellent oral, written, and listening abilities to communicate effectively with global healthcare clients.

Expert Preparation Guide for Wipro

1. SQL Mastery is Key

The JD specifically highlights "Simple & Complex SQL Queries." You must be highly comfortable with JOINS (Inner, Left, Right), GROUP BY, Aggregate Functions, and nested Subqueries. Practice writing queries on paper, as you might be asked to do so in the managerial round.

2. Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

Brush up on theoretical concepts. Know the difference between Agile and Waterfall methodologies. Be prepared to explain the various stages: Requirement Gathering, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance.

3. The Health Administration Domain

Wipro's Total Benefit Administration System deals with sensitive healthcare and HR data. Demonstrating an understanding of data accuracy, confidentiality, and high-quality testing in healthcare tech will significantly boost your profile.
