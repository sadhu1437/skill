Cashfree Payments - Building the Financial Infrastructure of Tomorrow

Location: Bellandur, Bengaluru | Work Model: On-Site

Drive Overview

Cashfree Payments is a leading payment and API banking solutions company, empowering Indian businesses to collect payments and make payouts seamlessly. We believe in our people and foster a culture that encourages finding new and creative ways to solve problems. We are currently seeking talented and driven individuals from the 2025 Batch to join our team in Bengaluru. As a Salesforce Engineer 1, you will work closely with our engineering, sales, and operations teams to build and support scalable CRM solutions that enhance business operations and customer experience.

Target Batch 2025 Graduates
Experience 0 - 1 Years
Department Engineering

Your Role & Responsibilities

This role is ideal for someone who is passionate about learning Salesforce technologies and building impactful, scalable solutions on the Salesforce platform.

  • Salesforce Development: Assist in designing, developing, and maintaining Salesforce solutions to support business requirements.
  • Feature Implementation: Work with senior engineers to develop features using Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), and Salesforce APIs.
  • Customization: Support the development and customization of Salesforce applications including Sales Cloud and Platform features.
  • Quality & Deployment: Participate in testing, debugging, and deployment of Salesforce applications and enhancements.
  • Process Improvement: Help identify opportunities to improve existing Salesforce processes and workflows.
  • Collaboration: Collaborate with cross-functional teams such as sales, operations, and product teams to understand requirements and implement solutions.
  • Troubleshooting: Troubleshoot basic technical issues related to Salesforce applications.
  • Best Practices: Participate in code reviews, documentation, and knowledge-sharing sessions, continuously staying updated with Salesforce capabilities.

Market Trend: Salesforce in Fintech

As fintech companies scale, managing B2B relationships, merchant onboarding, and sales pipelines becomes highly complex. Cashfree Payments leverages Salesforce to streamline these operations. A career starting as a Salesforce Engineer in a fast-paced fintech environment offers incredible growth. Mastering Apex and LWC positions you perfectly for high-demand, high-paying roles in cloud CRM architecture and enterprise software engineering.

Expert Preparation Guide for Cashfree Payments

1. Object-Oriented Programming & Fundamentals

The job description strongly emphasizes "proficiency in programming languages and strong OOPs concepts." Whether you use Java or JavaScript, be ready to explain Inheritance, Polymorphism, and Encapsulation with clear, practical examples. If you know Apex, demonstrate how it mirrors Java's object-oriented structure.

2. Salesforce Ecosystem Basics

Familiarize yourself with the core concepts of Salesforce, specifically Sales Cloud. Understand the difference between configuration (clicks) and customization (code using Apex/LWC). Know the basics of SOQL (Salesforce Object Query Language) and how data is structured (Objects, Records, Fields).

3. Web Technologies & APIs

Since Cashfree is an API-first payments company, integrating Salesforce with external systems is crucial. Brush up on your understanding of REST APIs, JSON parsing, and basic JavaScript (essential for Lightning Web Components).

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Trailhead Badges: If you don't have certifications, complete relevant modules on Salesforce Trailhead (e.g., Apex Basics, LWC Basics) and add your Trailblazer profile link to your resume.
  • Personal Project: Build a small Lightning Web Component that fetches and displays data from a mock REST API.
  • Understand Cashfree's Business: Read about Cashfree's products (Payment Gateway, Payouts, Secure ID). Understanding how a payment gateway operates will help you answer situational questions about CRM implementation.
