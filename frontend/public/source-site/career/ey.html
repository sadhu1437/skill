Analyst - Tech Consulting

EY (Ernst & Young) - Technology Strategy & Transformation

Req ID: 1651193 | Location: Mumbai (Primary Location Only)

Drive Overview

As a global leader in assurance, tax, transaction, and advisory services, EY is using the finance products, expertise, and systems developed to build a better working world. The IT Advisory practice at EY is looking for bright, analytical minds to join as an Analyst in the Technology Strategy & Transformation (TC) team in Mumbai. This is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for fresh graduates to dive into enterprise application development, process documentation, and SAP systems within a Big 4 consulting environment.

Experience Level 0 Years (Fresher)
Target Degree CS / IT / ECE
Core Skill OOP / Programming

Your Role & Responsibilities

An important part of your role will be to assist Seniors & Managers while actively participating within client engagements, translating business requirements into technical execution.

  • Application Development: Build and develop applications based on provided technical specifications and process documentation.
  • SAP Support: Support the design, build, and testing phases of complex SAP applications for global clients.
  • Problem Solving: Apply a practical approach to solving issues and complex problems, delivering insightful solutions.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work in a collaborative way to provide services across multiple client departments while adhering to commercial and legal requirements.

Eligibility & Qualifications

Object-Oriented Programming SAP Basics B.Tech/M.Tech Analytical Skills Presentation Skills
  • Education: Full-time Graduate or Postgraduates from recognized universities in disciplines of Computer Science (CS), Information Technology (IT), or Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE).
  • Experience: No prior experience required (Freshers are completely eligible).
  • Technical Skill: Good programming knowledge in at least 1 language, with a strong preference for Object-Oriented Programming (OOP).
  • Attributes: Highly analytical nature with the ability to grasp new concepts very quickly.
  • Soft Skills: Strong communication, facilitation, relationship-building, presentation, and negotiation skills.
  • Flexibility: Must be flexible to travel and willing to relocate if necessary.

Market Insight: Technology Strategy at Big 4

Consulting firms like EY are heavily investing in Technology Strategy & Transformation. Organizations worldwide are migrating to sophisticated ERPs like SAP to manage their digital core. Joining EY as a Tech Analyst gives you a dual advantage: you build hard coding and SAP skills while also developing high-level business consulting and client-facing communication skills. This unique blend makes professionals highly sought after in the global tech ecosystem.

Expert Preparation Guide for EY Tech Consulting

1. Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) Mastery

The JD specifically asks for OOP knowledge. Ensure you can clearly explain the four pillars: Encapsulation, Abstraction, Inheritance, and Polymorphism. Be ready to write pseudo-code or basic programs in Java, C++, or Python demonstrating these concepts.

2. Basics of Enterprise Software & SAP

Since you will support SAP design and build, spend an hour reading about what SAP ERP is, why large enterprises use it, and what ABAP (SAP's programming language) does. You don't need to be an expert, but showing awareness will highly impress your interviewers.

3. The Consulting Mindset (Case Studies)

EY values "analytical nature" and "relationship-building." You may be given a small situational case study (e.g., "A client's app is failing during peak load, how do you approach the problem?"). Structure your answer logically: identify the root cause, propose a technical solution, and explain how you would communicate this to the client.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Research EY's Culture: Read about EY's mission to "Build a better working world." Understand their flexible working arrangements and focus on inclusive environments.
  • Prepare your "Tell me about yourself": Tailor your introduction to highlight your programming projects and any team-leadership roles you took in college.
  • Aptitude Polish: Big 4 firms often conduct initial screening rounds focusing on quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and data interpretation. Keep your math skills sharp.
