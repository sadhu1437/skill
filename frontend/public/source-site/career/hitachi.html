IT Support Engineer

Hitachi Vantara - Powering the World's Innovators with Data

Location: Hyderabad / Pune | Req ID: R0132287

Drive Overview

We're Hitachi Vantara, the data foundation trusted by the world's innovators. Our resilient, high-performance data infrastructure means that customers—from global banks to theme parks like the Las Vegas Sphere—can focus on achieving the incredible. We are currently looking for passionate, tech-savvy professionals to join our internal IT team serving Hitachi Vantara employees. In this entry-level role, you will help simplify, modernise, and optimise our global infrastructure, working in a 24x7 collaborative environment.

Experience Level Entry-Level / Fresher
Primary Skills OS, DB, Scripting
Work Environment 24x7 Operations

Your Role & Responsibilities

As part of a 24x7 global infrastructure management team, you will support day-to-day operations and ensure an excellent user experience for internal stakeholders.

  • Infrastructure Support: Deploy, monitor, and maintain global IT infrastructure ensuring uninterrupted services.
  • Automation: Utilize programming and scripting languages to build new efficiencies and integrate emerging technologies into existing systems.
  • Problem Solving: Actively troubleshoot issues across Operating Systems (OS), Applications, and Databases.
  • Collaboration: Participate actively in daily huddles and communicate effectively through Mail and MS Teams.
  • Continuous Improvement: Maintain a go-getter attitude with a high appetite for learning new IT technologies and refining processes.

Market Trend: Intelligent IT Operations

The role of internal IT is rapidly shifting from manual "break-fix" ticketing to Intelligent IT Operations (AIOps) and Infrastructure as Code (IaC). Companies like Hitachi Vantara expect their support engineers to have strong scripting capabilities (Python/PowerShell) to automate repetitive deployment tasks. Mastering foundational Networking, OS, and Database concepts in this entry-level role creates a direct career pathway into high-paying DevOps, Cloud Architecture, and SRE (Site Reliability Engineering) positions.

Eligibility & Technical Requirements

Programming/Scripting Operating Systems Databases (SQL) Basic Networking Problem Solving
  • Technical Foundation: Good knowledge of OS (Windows/Linux), Applications, Databases, and Basic Networking (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP).
  • Scripting Skills: Strong programming/scripting skills (e.g., Python, Bash, or PowerShell) to aid in automation tasks.
  • Mindset: A self-starter with analytical and problem-solving skills, and a strong focus on helping customers.
  • Communication: Clear and effective communication skills for active participation in global meetings.
  • Flexibility: Willingness to work in a 24x7 global infrastructure management environment across Hyderabad or Pune.

Expert Preparation Guide for Hitachi Vantara

1. Scripting & Automation Focus

Hitachi uniquely emphasizes "strong programming/scripting skills" for a support role. You must be able to explain how you would write a Python or PowerShell script to automate a task, such as fetching system logs, checking server uptime, or querying a database for user details.

2. Core IT Infrastructure Knowledge

Brush up on your fundamentals. Be ready to explain the OSI model, how DNS resolution works, the difference between TCP and UDP, and basic commands for both Linux (e.g., grep, awk, chmod) and Windows (e.g., ipconfig, ping, tracert).

3. Behavioral & "Self-Starter" Mindset

Prepare examples using the STAR method of times when you proactively identified a problem and fixed it without being told. Highlighting your "appetite to learn" and involvement in tech communities or ERGs will align perfectly with Hitachi's collaborative culture.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Automate a Task: Create a simple script (e.g., a Python script that pings a list of websites and alerts if they are down) and host it on GitHub.
  • Database Refresher: Review basic SQL queries (SELECT, JOIN, WHERE) as understanding backend data flow is essential for troubleshooting application issues.
  • Learn about Hitachi Vantara: Research their work on the Las Vegas Sphere data infrastructure. Mentioning this during an interview shows you've done your homework.
