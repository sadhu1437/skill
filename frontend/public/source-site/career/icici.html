Probationary Officer (PO) Program

ICICI Bank - Exciting career opportunity for young Graduates & Engineers

Location: PAN India | Role: Relationship Manager (Sales)

Program Overview

The ICICI Bank Probationary Officer Program nurtures young professionals to pursue a highly rewarding career in banking, with a strong focus on learning, capability building, and cultural grounding. This initiative prepares participants to join the Bank as Relationship Managers in a sales role, guided by the Bank’s culture of “seva bhav”—serving customers with integrity, empathy, and responsibility.

The program includes 2 months of structured classroom training at a residential campus, the cost of which is fully borne by ICICI Bank. Upon successful completion of the training program, candidates are officially placed in Relationship Management roles at various dynamic locations across the country.

Stipend (During Training) ₹18,000 / Month
Starting CTC (Post Training) ₹5.00L - ₹5.50L / Year
Target Audience 0 - 2 Years Exp

Role of a Relationship Manager (Sales)

A Relationship Manager is a core anchor in taking ICICI's banking solutions and services to valuable customers involving a 360-degree banking approach.

  • Customer Acquisition: Responsible for acquiring new customer relationships and increasing the wallet share of existing customers with a resolute focus on service quality and customer delight.
  • Need Analysis: Engage closely with customers to understand their needs, build long-term relationships, and responsibly offer appropriate banking solutions.
  • Product Suite Management: Work on the whole suite of offerings including saving solutions, loan products, overdraft solutions, Insta loans, investment solutions, trade solutions, business loans, or wealth management solutions.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate and work with different internal teams to offer the best in class customer service.
  • Field Operations: The role involves continuous customer interactions and may require regular travel within and around the assigned location or city.
  • Core Philosophy: Uphold the fundamental value of being "Fair to the Bank and Fair to the Customer" (Truth, Trust, and Transparency).

Strict Eligibility Criteria

Age: Below 25 Years 60%+ in 10th & Grad Graduates & Engineers 0 to 2 Years Exp Locationally Mobile
  • Age Limit: Applicants must be strictly below 25 years of age (as on the date of application).
  • Academics: Must have secured 60% and above in both 10th standard and Graduation.
  • Educational Background: Open to Graduates, Engineers, and Post Graduates from recognized universities.
  • Experience: Freshers (0 years) up to candidates with a maximum of 2 years of work experience are eligible.
  • Mobility & Role Readiness: Applicants MUST be locationally mobile and explicitly willing to undertake Relationship & Sales roles involving field travel.

Selection Process Roadmap

ICICI Bank follows a streamlined, two-step evaluation process for the PO Program:

Step 1: Online Aptitude Test

Once shortlisted, applicants undergo a comprehensive online aptitude test comprising four major sections: Verbal Ability, Quantitative Ability, Logical Reasoning, and General Awareness.

Step 2: Personal Interview (Face-to-Face)

Applicants who successfully clear the aptitude test will be invited for an in-person interview. This round focuses heavily on communication skills, sales acumen, and cultural alignment with ICICI's core values.

Expert Preparation Guide for ICICI PO Program

1. Acing the Aptitude Test

The "General Awareness" section heavily focuses on current affairs, banking terminology (Repo Rate, CRR, SLR, NEFT, RTGS), and recent economic trends. Practice standard Bank PO level questions for Quant, Logical, and Verbal reasoning using platforms like Indiabix.

2. The "Sales & Seva Bhav" Mindset

This is explicitly a Sales and Relationship Management role. During the interview, you MUST demonstrate enthusiasm for sales, target achievements, and client interactions. Use the STAR method to explain a time you convinced someone, solved a customer problem, or handled a target-driven task.

3. Understanding the 360-Degree Banking Approach

Familiarize yourself with retail banking products: CASA (Current Account Savings Account), Fixed Deposits, Mutual Funds, Life Insurance, and Overdrafts. Be ready to participate in a mock scenario where you cross-sell a product to an existing customer based on their financial needs.
