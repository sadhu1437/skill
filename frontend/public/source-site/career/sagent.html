IT Support Engineer - Trainee

Sagent - Transforming the Mortgage Servicing Industry

Location: Chennai, India | Shift: 24/7 Rotational

Drive Overview

Sagent is a joint venture that combines Fiserv Inc.'s decades of market-leading fintech expertise with Warburg Pincus' skill in growing technology companies. We are transforming the mortgage servicing industry by bringing the modern experience customers expect from loan originations to loan servicing. We are looking for motivated and tech-savvy freshers to join our Chennai team as an IT Support Engineer – Trainee. If you are a quick learner eager to kick-start your career in IT support, Sagent is the place where you will make an impact from day one!

Role Trainee / Fresher
Target Degree BE / B.Tech (IT/CS/EC)
Work Mode On-site (Chennai)

Your Day-to-Day at Sagent

You will play a crucial part in providing excellent technical support to our customers and internal teams, ensuring a stable and well-performing system environment.

  • Service Desk Operations: Work actively as a part of the IT service desk, logging, tracking, and resolving tickets in a timely manner.
  • Technical Troubleshooting: Provide basic and intermediate troubleshooting for Windows devices, Laptops, Monitors, and CPUs.
  • Network & App Support: Assist users with ISP connectivity issues, Smart Phones, E-mail clients, MS Office Applications, and web browsers.
  • System Optimization: Apply professional competencies to administer, monitor, and optimize the assigned system environments.
  • Communication: Deliver exceptional service through excellent verbal and written communication with internal teams and customers.

Expert Preparation Guide for IT Support Trainee

1. Hardware & OS Basics

Sagent emphasizes troubleshooting Windows devices. Ensure you know how to use the Task Manager, Event Viewer, Command Prompt (ping, ipconfig, tracert), and how to resolve common Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) errors.

2. MS Office & Browser Troubleshooting

As a service desk engineer, you will deal with Outlook sync issues, Excel crashes, and browser cache/cookie problems. Brush up on how to configure an email client (IMAP/POP/Exchange) and how to manage browser extensions.

3. The Customer Service Mindset

The role requires "excellent verbal and written communication." In your interview, use the STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to explain a time you successfully helped a frustrated peer or customer solve a technical problem patiently.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Familiarize with Ticketing Tools: Read up on how IT Service Management (ITSM) tools like Jira Service Desk or ServiceNow operate.
  • Learn Active Directory (AD): Watch a tutorial on basic AD tasks like resetting user passwords and unlocking accounts, as this is a day-to-day task in IT Support.
