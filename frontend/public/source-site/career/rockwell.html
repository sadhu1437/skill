Graduate Engineer Trainee

Rockwell Automation - Powering Sustainable Industrial Innovation

Location: Pune, India | Work Model: Hybrid

⚠️ Final Call: Applications close on May 9th, 2026 (Only 14 hours left)

Drive Overview

Rockwell Automation is a global technology leader with over 28,000 employees dedicated to helping the world’s manufacturers become more productive, sustainable, and agile. As a Graduate Engineer Trainee based in Pune, you will join the Business Operations pillar. You will work on roadmap features, technical debt, and product health issues reported by global customers, contributing to life-saving medical production and green mobility infrastructures.

Target Batch 2025 & 2026 Freshers
Min. CGPA 7.0 and Above
Primary Stack C# / ASP.NET (MS Stack)

What You Will Do

This role offers a structured campus-to-corporate transition where you will learn the end-to-end Agile SDLC.

  • Software Engineering: Write production-grade code using the Microsoft Tech Stack (C#, ASP.NET).
  • Agile Execution: Follow Agile methodologies and utilize modern tools throughout the development cycle.
  • System Analysis: Analyze and design software programs for feature enhancements and new product launches.
  • Documentation: Complete professional documentation for system installation and maintenance procedures.
  • Customer Success: Support the pillar in resolving complex product health issues reported by industrial clients.
  • Teamwork: Collaborate with cross-cultural teams to meet strictly integrated technical solutions.

Eligibility & Technical Foundation

  • Education: B.E/B.Tech in Computer Science, IT, or related technical fields.
  • Preferred Credentials: Candidates who have completed the **CDAC course** will receive high priority.
  • Database: Hands-on experience with SQL Server, Oracle, or relational RDBMS.
  • Frameworks: Familiarity with ASP.NET, node.js, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
  • Version Control: Experience with Git or Subversion is essential.
  • Citizenship: Must have valid work authorization for India.

Rockwell Selection Process Roadmap

1. Initial Resume & CGPA Check

Shortlisting based on the 7.0 CGPA cut-off and 2025/2026 graduation status.

2. Technical Assessment (Online)

A rigorous test covering C# OOPs fundamentals, SQL query logic, and basic Web Development scenarios.

3. Technical Interview (MS Stack Focus)

Deep dive into your CDAC/college projects. Expect questions on ASP.NET lifecycle, Dependency Injection, and SQL performance tuning.

4. Managerial & HR Round

Final evaluation of work ethics, collaborative mindset, and your interest in the industrial automation sector.

Expert Preparation Guide for Rockwell

1. Mastery of C# & OOPs

Rockwell relies on robust, scalable backend systems. Master the pillars of Object-Oriented Programming: Inheritance, Polymorphism, Abstraction, and Encapsulation with C# syntax.

2. SQL Server & Data Modeling

Be prepared for questions on **Normalisation, Indexing, and Stored Procedures**. In industrial automation, data integrity and retrieval speed are critical.

3. The Industrial Context

Research "Industry 4.0" and "Digital Twins." Showing awareness of how software impacts the factory floor will significantly impress the engineering managers.
