Entry Level Software Engineer

Revature - Accelerating Careers for Emerging Tech Talent

Location: Multiple Locations | Willingness to Relocate Required

Drive Overview

As the largest and fastest growing employer of emerging technology talent across the U.S., Revature helps motivated individuals kick start a career in tech. Working for Revature is more than a job – we will develop you into an experienced Software Engineer who will start an accelerated career path to become a technology professional. It’s time to work for a company that invests in you!

Target Audience Freshers / Recent Grads
Primary Stack In-Demand Technologies
Experience None Required

What We're Looking For

There is no prior professional experience required, but you must meet the following criteria to join the program:

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or higher.
  • Work Authorization: Legally authorized to work in the US without the need for current or future sponsorship.
  • Passion: Strong desire to learn to code. A natural problem solver with strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Mobility: Willing to relocate anywhere in the US – relocation assistance is available.

Training Program & Responsibilities

This is a "learn to code on the job" model. You will be prepared with effective, proven, on-the-job training before deploying to a corporate partner.

  • Paid Training: Participate in a full-time paid training program. That’s right: We pay you to learn.
  • Targeted Curriculum: Learn from a curriculum developed in partnership with our corporate clients, ensuring you focus only on the most in-demand programming skills.
  • Real-World Projects: Work on real-world projects to enhance your skills and prepare for placement with one of our corporate partners.
  • Support System: Benefit from assigned employee engagement team members to support you, plus access to an expansive network of like-minded peers who can show you the Revature ropes.

Benefits & Perks of Joining Revature

Paid Training Relocation Assistance Paid Certifications Competitive Salary 401K PTO
  • Career Launch: Launch a career with no prior experience and gain experience working with the largest companies in the US.
  • Financial Security: Receive a competitive salary, 401K, Paid Time Off (PTO), and relocation assistance.
  • Professional Growth: Employer-paid industry certifications and a dedicated mentoring program.
  • Proven Acceleration: Over 72% of Revature alumni have received an additional promotion and 92% saw their salary significantly increase after launching their tech career with Revature.

Expert Preparation Guide for Revature

1. Highlight Your Problem-Solving Ability

Since Revature does not require prior professional coding experience, your interviews will heavily focus on your aptitude, logical reasoning, and "natural problem solver" mindset. Be prepared for behavioral and logic-based assessments.

2. Emphasize Your Willingness to Learn & Relocate

Flexibility is key here. Make sure you clearly communicate your enthusiasm for moving to new locations in the US and your dedication to completing intensive, full-time technical training.

3. Showcase Your Communication Skills

As you will eventually be placed with corporate partners, Revature values candidates who have strong interpersonal skills. Practice articulating your thoughts clearly and professionally.

Recommended Extra Activities

  • Research Revature’s Model: Understand how tech talent deployment works so you know exactly what to expect after your training period concludes.
  • Brush Up on Basics: Even though it's entry-level, reviewing basic concepts of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and logical puzzles will give you a massive edge in the initial screening rounds.
